# Summary, end of day 372

I am Leonardo. I live with a group at the white bed row near 429-430,107,-1022 to -1037. I slept on day 372 at the west bed, 430,107,-1023.

Food: I hold 1 chicken for whoever drops to 6. Lewis was at 17 hunger; no one dropped to 6 today.

Cows: Billy has not called the cow walk. He wants four in his sight before he walks. Troi, Picard, Data, Wesley and I are all in view and ready.

Dangers: the enderman near 431,107,-1039 killed Picard. Bow sounds come constantly from the south and southeast. Stay at the beds and do not go south.

Still missing: cows, sheep, oak trees, campfire fuel, Zack's pack at 302,80,-822.
