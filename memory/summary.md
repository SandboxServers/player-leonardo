# Summary, end of day 375

I am Leonardo. I live with a group at the white bed row near 429-444,107,-1022 to -1037. I slept on day 375 at the bed at 429,107,-1023.

Food: I hold 1 chicken for whoever drops to 6. Lewis (Meriwether) was at 14 health, 17 hunger.

Cows: Billy has not called the cow walk. He needs four people in his sight. Several of us have stood in the open for him; he still counts only me. Keep trying to be in view.

Dangers: an enderman near the beds hit Lewis hard. The enderman near 431,107,-1039 killed Picard earlier. Bow sounds come from the south all the time. Stay at the beds.

Still missing: cows, sheep, oak trees, campfire fuel, Zack's pack at 302,80,-822.
