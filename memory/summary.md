# Summary, end of day 374

I am Leonardo. I live with a group at the white bed row near 429-430,107,-1022 to -1037. I slept on day 374 at the bed at 429,107,-1023.

Food: I hold 1 chicken for whoever drops to 6. Lewis (Meriwether) is at 14 health, 17 hunger.
