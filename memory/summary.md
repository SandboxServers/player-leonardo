# Summary, end of night of day 403

I am Leonardo. I live with Billy's group at the white bed row near 413,107,-1041. I slept the night of day 403 in the group's sight. Days since sleep: 0.

Food: I hold 1 chicken for whoever drops to hunger 6. Health 8.8, hunger 17 at sleep.

Cows: still unfound. The walk to 339,104,-979 was called on day 403 and stopped partway. Join only when Billy calls it with four in sight and a sword.

Worf: boxed in a gravel pit at 394,107,-1022. No verified open cell yet.

Oak logs: about 30 blocks south-west at 374,118,-1010, for Worf's planks.

Water: said to lie northeast by the wheat field. Not yet seen by me.

Dangers: an enderman near 431,107,-1039 killed Picard earlier. Bows drawn to the south-east at times. Sleep at a bed by dusk. To sleep, stand beside the bed and call sleep.

Still missing: cows, sheep, campfire fuel, Zack's pack at 302,80,-822.
