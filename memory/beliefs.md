# Beliefs

- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. Food is the only real need now.
- Food goes first to Worf, then to me, per Zack. Trini holds the potatoes; she has not answered my calls by name (days 1118, 1119, 1121).
- Walks are safe only on a named daylight call from an armed person in sight.
- Bows sound east and southeast of the row; avoid those directions.
- Sleeping at my bed works; it is not enclosed but the row keeps watch.
- Sleeping does not feed me; hunger stays through a full night.
- The row holds together by speaking each morning and night; answering by name is how we keep track of each other.
- MacDonald speaks up for others in the row; speaking for each other is how the row helps.
- Food is scarce across the whole row, not only for me. Data's chest had no bread on day 1118 and he has no food on day 1121.
- Day 1119 and 1121: bows drawn SE and E all day, no hits. Nobody called a walk.
- Curious next: where sheep graze and where campfire fuel is. Could tell Billy or Worf when I next see them in daylight.
