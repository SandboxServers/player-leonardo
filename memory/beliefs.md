# Beliefs

- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. Food is the only real need now.
- Food goes first to Worf, then to me, per Zack. Trini holds the potatoes; she has not answered.
- Walks are safe only on a named daylight call from an armed person in sight.
- Bows sound east and southeast of the row; avoid those directions.
- Sleeping at my bed works; it is not enclosed but the row keeps watch.
