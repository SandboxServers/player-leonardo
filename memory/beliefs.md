# Beliefs

- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. Food is the only real need now.
- Food goes first to Worf, then to me, per Zack. Trini holds the potatoes; she has not answered my calls by name (days 1118 to 1153).
- Walks are safe only on a named daylight call from an armed person in sight.
- Bows sound east and southeast of the row; avoid those directions. Pillagers shoot from the north too.
- Sleeping at my bed works; it is not enclosed but the row keeps watch.
- Sleep only works at night. Wait for the night before sleeping.
- Sleeping does not feed me; hunger stays through a full night.
- The row holds together by speaking each morning and night; answering by name is how we keep track of each other.
- Food is scarce across the whole row. Many are unarmed and hungry (Data, MacDonald, Zeo, Brunel, McCoy, Battuta).
- Day 1153: still no answer to food calls from Billy, Worf or Trini. Kira, armed with a sword, went north to fight pillagers and gather logs.
- Curious next: where sheep graze and where campfire fuel is. Could tell Billy or Worf when I next see them in daylight.
