# Beliefs

- Sleep is refused in daylight ("it is not night"). At night it can be refused too while monsters are near, even with none in view; then wait and try again.
- Pillagers raid from the south of the row and kill the unarmed.
- Dying wakes you at the row spawn with nothing; the group shares what it has.
- Billy names the walks; the group waits for his call. Walks can be called off at dusk.
- The west path past x=370 is blocked from the row; Wesley's camp at 416,107,-1050 was not found.
- Skeleton horses roam south-west of the row.
- Food is the limit; no sheep seen yet.
