# Beliefs

- Beds: clicking a bed with use fails ("that block is a bed"). Stand beside it and call sleep; that worked at dusk.
- Sleep works in the row's sight with the group; it takes some time to fully take.
- Hunger stays low and does not regenerate health; food is the hard limit. Feed the lowest first.
- Billy calls the cow walk only when hunger nears 6 and with a sword in hand.
- Phantoms and bows are the danger at night and in the northeast; stay east of them and near the row.
