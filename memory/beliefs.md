# Beliefs

- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. Food is the only real need now.
- Food goes first to the hungriest: Scotty and Seven at day 1157. Worf and I are next after them.
- Walks are safe only on a named daylight call from an armed person in sight (Billy, Worf or Kira).
- Bows sound east and southeast of the row; avoid those directions. Pillagers shoot from the north too.
- Sleeping at a white bed in the group's sight works. Sleep only works at night, and sleeping does not feed me.
- The row keeps together by speaking each morning and at dusk; answering by name is how we keep track of each other.
- Food is scarce across the whole row. Han has 8 oak logs and planks; Watney has 8 logs and 6 planks.
- Melons were reported near 389,-1156 (Rocky and Amelia plan a walk there in daylight).
- Curious next: where sheep graze and where campfire fuel is. Tell Billy or Worf when I see them in daylight.
