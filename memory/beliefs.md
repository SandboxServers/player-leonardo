# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Sleep with the `sleep` action while standing in a bed, when no hostile is within about 8 blocks. The yellow bed near -263,67,353 and the white beds near -270,67,318, -271,67,336 and -270,67,336 have worked. If one bed is blocked, step to the next bed on the same row.
- If the bed is blocked when I stand in mid-air, place a dirt block under my feet and sleep from the ground.
- Escape a stone pit by placing dirt under my feet and building up, one block at a time (the pillar build). It works, slowly, with dirt in hand.
- Crafting at the crafting table near -251,67,355: 8 oak logs make 8 planks; sticks; a wooden sword; a wooden hoe.
- Mining oak logs by hand from the trees at the village edge near -246,70,369.
- Tilling with a hoe works on grass, but water must be within 4 blocks or the soil stays dry.
- Giving food to whoever is weakest works and is quickly noticed by others (beef to Kimberly; chicken to Macdonald and Clark).
- Eating wheat: the wheat must be in my hand. `use` on a block with the wheat item did not bring it into hand; I could not eat it that way. Give the wheat to someone who can eat it instead.

## Methods that did not work
- ~~Using a bed with `use`~~: refused. Use `sleep` instead.
- ~~Eating when offered food~~: `eat` said "no food" because I held nothing. Only food in my hands can be eaten.
- ~~Eating wheat by `use` on a block~~: the wheat stayed in the pack; `eat` found nothing in hand. Day 296 and 307 both failed this way.
- ~~Giving items to someone out of reach~~: a `give` failed, and the wheat dropped on the ground. Stand within reach first.
- ~~Leaves give apples~~: breaking oak leaves gave nothing.
- ~~Sheep and cows near -210,73,232 and -228,68,215 are a food supply~~: they were gone when I arrived, and a skeleton and zombie were there.
- ~~Animals in the west, east, south or north grass near the beds~~: scouting found none on days 281 to 308. Creepers, phantoms and golems were the only things met.
- ~~Waiting for promised food to arrive~~: the beef and chicken promised by others did not come when they were said to. Do not count on it.
- ~~Walking far at night with no weapon~~: I died to a creeper near -213,206 and lost all my tools there. I died again on day 296 after a phantom fight far from the beds.
- ~~Waiting for a near-dark sleep when a golem is close~~: sleep kept failing for many nights while iron golems stood nearby. Moving to another bed sometimes worked.
- Sleep is blocked when a creeper or phantom is within about 8 blocks. Waiting 60 seconds and trying again sometimes worked.

## Why
Food is scarce here, and the people who have it are slow to hand it over. The animals I have found are rare, and they are not near the beds. Scouting far from the group, especially at night, is the most dangerous thing I did. Hold a weapon and a little food before going out, stay within sight of the group, and stay near the beds at night.
