# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Sleep with the `sleep` action while standing in a bed, when no hostile is within about 8 blocks. The village bed near -263,67,353 and the white beds near -270,67,318 and -270,67,336 have worked.
- Crafting at the crafting table near -251,67,355: 8 oak logs make 8 planks; sticks; a wooden sword; a wooden hoe.
- Mining oak logs by hand from the trees at the village edge near -246,70,369.
- Tilling with a hoe works on grass (dry farmland at -262,65,320 and -266,65,322), but water must be within 4 blocks or the soil stays dry.
- Giving food to whoever is weakest works and is quickly noticed by others (beef to Kimberly).

## Why
Food is scarce here, and the people who have it are slow to hand it over. Scouting far from the group in the dark is the most dangerous thing I did. Hold a weapon and a little food before going out, and stay near the beds at night.
