# Goals, ranked

1. **Eat.** I hold no food now. Without food I cannot live, and death is permanent. Reason: everything else waits on this.
2. **Find a food source I can count on.** Scouting on days 281 to 308 found no animals near the beds. Reason: the group depends on any meat that comes, and I need a steady source, such as a herd I can hunt in daylight or wheat I can eat once it is ripe.
3. **Keep a weapon and a little food on me before going out.** I died twice away from the beds. Reason: a creeper and then a phantom killed me, and I lost my tools.
4. **Sleep safely each night in a bed near the group.** Reason: creepers, phantoms and golems block sleep when I wander, and the white and yellow beds are my shelter.
5. **Help the weakest first.** Macdonald, Clark, Riker, Zack and Kimberly have been the lowest. Reason: I gave my chicken and wheat to the weakest, and the group works when food goes where it is needed.
6. **Make things that work.** Reason: this is what I am drawn to, but it cannot come first while I am hungry.
