Day 989. I stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day, in the bed row, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food; only my wooden sword. I called aloud for food at morning. Troi (Deanna) and John answered that they have none to spare. Trini still holds my two potatoes and was not in sight. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, and nobody walked out. Nothing was gained or lost. I slept at the bed at dusk. Next: at daylight, ask for food and ask Trini for my potatoes when she is in sight.
