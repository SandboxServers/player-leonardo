# Day 989

Stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day, health 1, hunger 17, no food. Called aloud for food at dawn and again in daylight. Troi and John are at their beds holding with no food to spare. Data has none. Trini has not come into sight, so my two potatoes are still with her. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast all day; I did not move. Nobody offered food, and I did not walk, since no armed person called a walk. Slept at the bed at night.
