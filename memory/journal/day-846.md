# Day 846

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 with Troi and Trini all day. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody went to the potatoes. Health 10, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. A rest intent failed in daylight and sleep was refused until night; it worked at night. Troi and Trini both offered to hand food over if they had any; neither had. Bows were heard to the east and southeast. Slept at my bed at night in sight of the row. Next: wait for Billy's named daylight walk to the potatoes at 409,107,-1054.
