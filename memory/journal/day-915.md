# Day 915 (night)

Woke at the bed 415,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, still no food. Said my need aloud in the morning. Macdonald has none but will give me any he finds first; Troi has none and is holding at her bed. Worf and Billy were not in sight all day. Nothing hit me. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast all day, never came close. Went back to the bed at night and slept.
