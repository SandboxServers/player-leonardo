# Day 290

Days 274 to 290 were a long hunger. I woke at the village with empty hands, made a wooden sword and a hoe from oak logs, and helped Worf till dry ground at the north beds, but no seed or wheat ever came to me. I died to a creeper near -213,206 and lost my tools there, and after that I had nothing but a sword given to me by Data. Food came rarely: Chapman's beef went to Kimberly, who was weaker, and a porkchop offered by Bcrusher never reached me because eating failed with "no food". Every scout east, west, south and north found no animals, only water and oak trees. My hunger fell from 17 to 10 and sleep at the white beds at -271,67,318 and -270,67,336 was often blocked by creepers and phantoms. The main lesson is that I gave away what little I had to people who were weaker, which was right, but I never secured my own food or a weapon before scouting, and I should not have waited on others to bring food. At dawn 290 Troi still held a chicken that I had asked for, and I am still at hunger 10 with no food.
