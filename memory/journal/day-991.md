# Day 991

Woke at bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Asked aloud for bread or a potato; none came. Troi and MacDonald have no food to spare. Trini, who holds my two potatoes, was not in sight all day. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east several times, but nothing came near. No walk was named, so I stayed at the bed. Went to sleep at dusk in the bed row, not enclosed. Nothing new learned except that the bow sounds keep coming from the same two directions.
