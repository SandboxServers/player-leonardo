# Day 642

Quiet day at the row. Woke at bed 402,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, with only my wooden sword. Said good morning and held in Billy's sight. Heard the row hold through the morning; nobody named a walk, and nobody has seen sheep yet. Dusk came and I slept at my bed; the first sleep was partial (5 of 10 needed asleep), and the second worked. Nothing was won or lost today. Still no food in my pack, so food stays the limit.
