Day 871. Woke at bed 416,107,-1023, health 11, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Nobody came to name a walk. Trini held at 430,107,-1023 and said she would not walk in the dark. Bows were heard to the southeast and east all day, so nobody went out. I said good morning and asked Billy about the potato walk; no answer came. Slept at my bed at dusk.
