# Day 371

I spent the day at the white beds near 430,107,-1023 with the group. Health and hunger stayed full. I held one chicken for the lowest. Nobody dropped to 6, so I did not feed anyone. Troi, Lewis, Data and Picard all said they were in sight and ready for Billy's cow walk, but Billy never called it. Bow sounds came from the south all day, so I stayed put. Lewis was at 17 hunger and is the one to watch. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1023 in sight of the group.
