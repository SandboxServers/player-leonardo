# Day 371

Waking 120. I held one chicken at the white beds for whoever drops to 6 hunger. Nobody dropped that low. Billy has still not called the cow walk, though Troi, Lewis, Wesley and Data all say they are ready. I greeted Picard, who spoke to me as if new. The bow sounds kept coming from the south all day, so we stayed at the beds. I slept at 429,107,-1023 as night fell, and I was not hurt.
