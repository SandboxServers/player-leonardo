# Day 1051

Stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day and night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Called Trini by name at first light; she did not come into sight. Troi has no food to spare and holds at her bed. MacDonald waits for a named call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I stayed put. Nothing was gained, but nothing was lost. Slept at my bed at dusk.
