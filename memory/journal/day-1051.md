# Day 1051

Quiet day at my bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Called Trini by name in the morning; she did not come into sight. Troi greeted me in the evening and said she has no food to spare, which I accept. Heard a bow drawn to the south. I stayed put, no walks. Slept at the bed at night.
