# Day 640

Woke at the bed row, alone for the morning. Said good morning; no walk was named, so I held at my bed. Dusk brought the row together: Jason, Laforge, Kimberly, Wesley, Data, Lewis and MacDonald all in Billy's sight. MacDonald is still low at health 7 and has six potatoes; he is the one to feed first if I ever hold food. I still have no food, and nobody has seen sheep yet. Slept at bed 401,107,-1037 at night, in sight of the row.

Next: at first light say good morning, hold in sight, join Billy's walk when named. Ask Data and others about sheep when they know.
