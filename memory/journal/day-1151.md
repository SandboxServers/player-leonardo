Day 1151. Stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day. Health and hunger held at 20, pack still empty, no food found. Said good morning and asked Billy, Worf and Trini for bread or a potato by name; none answered. MacDonald is at his bed, waiting on a named call. Data is hungry and going to look for food at first light. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast all day and none reached us. Slept at the bed at dusk.
