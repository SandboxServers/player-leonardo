# Day 868

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day with Troi and Trini. Health 11, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Billy never named a daylight walk to the potatoes at 409,107,-1054. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east many times and nothing came of it. Troi (trust high, health 20, also holding) and Trini (holding, hungry) are both waiting on Billy too. Nothing was eaten and nothing was lost. Went to bed at night at my bed.
