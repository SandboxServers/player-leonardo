# Day 375

Held at the white bed row at 429,107,-1023 all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me, Lewis was at 14 health and 17 hunger. Billy never called the cow walk: he sees only me in his sight, so he holds until four stand in view. Several of us stepped into the open for him. An enderman hit Lewis near the beds and he backed off west to the bed row. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night, never close. I held my chicken for whoever drops to 6. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1023 at night.
