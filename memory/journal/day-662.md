# Day 662

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 with a wooden sword and no food, hunger 20, health 20. Nobody at the row had a walk named, so we all held in Billy's sight. Data has still seen no sheep, only cows far east at 339,104,-979. MacDonald and Troi are thin, and Worf will not take a potato without Billy's word. I said good morning, asked about sheep, and held. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Nothing new to act on yet; still curious where sheep graze.
