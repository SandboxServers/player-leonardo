# Day 1090

Quiet night at the white bed 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. MacDonald (health 20) stayed at the bed nearby and said goodnight to the row. Nobody gave food. Trini has not answered my request for a potato. Nothing was done beyond holding at the bed. Tomorrow: at daylight say good morning, ask once by name for food, and walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is still owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
