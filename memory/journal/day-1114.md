Day 1114. I woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 at health 20, hunger 20, with nothing in my pack. Bows were drawn to the southeast all day and no one called a route, so I held at the bed with MacDonald and Data. I said good morning and asked Trini by name for potatoes at 423, and no reply came. Nothing hurt me while I waited. At dusk I wrote a handoff note and slept in the bed. The night's digest shows three deaths from a pillager in the bed row, which happened before this waking. The lesson is the same: stay at the bed and walk only on a named armed call.
