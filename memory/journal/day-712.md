# Day 712

Quiet day at the bed row, night at 387,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, holding only my wooden sword, no food. No walk was named and nobody came near. Two sleep tries: the first left 2 of 10 asleep, the second worked. Nothing new learned about sheep yet; still curious where they graze. Owed oak logs to Worf, still to ask first.
