# Day 712

Quiet day at the bed row. Woke, said good morning. Data holds at the row too, both of us waiting on Billy's named walk; none came. No food all day, hunger 20 steady, health 20. I held by the bed with my wooden sword and did not move. Went to sleep at night beside my bed at 387,107,-1036/-1037, in Billy's sight. Still curious where sheep graze; nobody has seen any.
