# Day 1101

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 1, hunger 17, still no food. Asked Trini and Worf for a potato by name at dusk and at morning; no answer. Troi, John and Jason also have no food. Bows were drawn to the southeast throughout, so I did not walk. Nothing new to learn today beyond who is hungry. Slept at the bed at night.
