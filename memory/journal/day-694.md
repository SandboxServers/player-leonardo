# Day 694

Waking at the row, I said good morning and held at my bed with my wooden sword. No walk was named all day; Billy stayed quiet while the others waited. Health and hunger stayed at 20 and 20. At dusk the row went to bed, and I slept at my bed 402,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Nothing new was learned today, and no food was found. Sheep are still unseen.
