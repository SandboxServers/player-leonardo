# Day 945

Woke at the bed row, health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Said aloud for food several times. Nobody in the row had food to give; Trini still has not handed over her two potatoes. Bows were drawn to the southeast through the day, so I stayed in the row and did not walk. My rest intent kept failing at once, so I switched to plain waits. At dusk I slept at the bed 415,107,-1023. Nothing was gained today except staying alive.
