# Day 1008

Still at white bed 416,107,-1023, health 1, hunger 17, no food. Asked aloud for food several times. Troi, MacDonald and Data have none; Zack says Trini holds 2 potatoes for him. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day and I stayed put in the bed row. Nothing walked, nothing lost. Went to bed at dusk with the handoff written.
