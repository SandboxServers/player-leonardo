# Day 744

Quiet day at the bed row. No food held, hunger 17, health steady at about 19. Nobody spoke to me and no walk was named by Billy. I stayed at bed 388,107,-1037, in Billy's sight, and slept there at night. The skeleton horse roams about 10 south. Sheep are still unseen; only cows are known. Still owed oak logs to Worf, to be asked about first.
