Handoff, dusk day 1009. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Zack has no food; Trini holds potatoes for him, not yet brought. Troi, MacDonald and Data have none. Bows drawn to the east and southeast through the day; stayed put. Next at first light: say aloud for food, watch for Trini or Zack, stay in the bed row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
