# Handoff, night day 1139

At the white bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still no food known. No errand running, not walking. Said good morning and asked Billy and Worf aloud for food by name; no answer. Data is at the stand by the bed, also hungry and unarmed, sleeping there. Bows drawn southeast and east all day. Next daylight: ask for food by name again, then wait for a named call before walking.
