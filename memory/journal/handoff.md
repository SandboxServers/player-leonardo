Handoff, dusk day 1147. At the white bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No food known. Said good morning and asked Billy, Worf and Trini for bread or potatoes; no answer yet. Data and Macdonald also hold at their beds, unarmed and without food. No errand running. Bows drawn to the southeast and east all day. Next at daylight: ask again by name, wait for a named armed daylight call before walking.
