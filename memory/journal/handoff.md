At the white bed 416,107,-1023, in the row's sight, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Only a wooden sword.
Said good morning and asked Trini and Worf for potato. No answer yet. John, Jason and Troi also have no food.

No errands out. No walks. Bows sounding to the south and southeast.
