Handoff, day 1018 dusk. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Data and Troi have none; MacDonald holds at his bed for Billy or Worf. Trini and Zack (potatoes) not seen. Bows drawn to the southeast all day; stayed put. Next at first light: say for food, watch for Trini, Zack or Billy, stay in the bed row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
