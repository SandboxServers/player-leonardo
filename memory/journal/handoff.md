Handoff, day 999 dusk, going to sleep at bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row. Not enclosed, no errand, no walk running, nobody hostile in sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Asked aloud for food this morning; Troi and MacDonald have none spare. Trini (holds my two potatoes) still not in sight. Bows heard to the east and southeast all day; stay at the bed. Next: at first light say aloud for food again, and wait for a named daylight call before any walk.
