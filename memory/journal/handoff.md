Handoff, day 1041 night. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Said need to Trini, Billy, Troi, MacDonald by name; no potato came yet. Troi says Trini was asked to bring potatoes. Bows heard SE and E all day, none nearby. Next at first light: say need once more, wait for food. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
