Handoff, day 1029 night. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed, not in danger. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Asked aloud for one potato many times; Trini no reply; Data, MacDonald, Troi have none. Bows drawn SE and E all day. Next at first light: say for one potato, hold in the row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person. Sleeping at the bed now.
