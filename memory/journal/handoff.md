# Handoff, day 1063 night

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17 (steady), no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. John and MacDonald are holding at the bed row for the night.

Called Trini, Billy and Worf by name at dusk. No reply yet.

Next: at first light, call Trini by name in daylight, in sight. Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
