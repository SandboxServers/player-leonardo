# Handoff, day 1110 night

At white bed 416,107,-1023, holding, not enclosed but in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand half-done. Bows drawn to the southeast and east.

Asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato at dawn; MacDonald also asked them to give me one first. No answer yet. Jason and Troi have no food either.

Next: at daylight, say good morning and ask once more for a potato. Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
