# Handoff, day 954 (dusk, going to sleep)

At bed 415,107,-1023 in the bed row. No errand running, not in danger as far as I know.
Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Wooden sword only.
Asked the row aloud for food at morning and midday; nobody has food (Troi, MacDonald, Billy, Data). Trini still owes me two potatoes.
Bows heard to the east and southeast all day; not seen. Stayed in the row.
On waking: say aloud for food, eat at once if any arrives, stay in the row. No walks without a named daylight call from an armed person.
