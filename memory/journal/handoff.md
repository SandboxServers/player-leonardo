Handoff, night of day 1096.
At the white bed 416,107,-1023, in the row's sight, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Only a wooden sword.
Said good morning and asked for potatoes by name. No food for me yet; Troi has none, Billy and Trini hold the potatoes.
No errands out. No walks. Bows sound to the southeast and east.
Next: at daylight, say good morning, ask once more for food. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
