Handoff, day 1014. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Said aloud for food several times; Troi, MacDonald, Data, Worf have none. Trini and Zack not seen. Bows drawn to the southeast and east all day; stayed put. Next at first light: say for food, watch for Trini or Zack, stay in the bed row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
