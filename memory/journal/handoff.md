# Handoff, day 953 (night, slept)

At bed 415,107,-1023 in the bed row. Slept the night. No errand running, not in danger as far as I know.
Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Wooden sword only.
Asked the row aloud for food at day, dusk and morning; nobody has food. Troi, MacDonald and Billy have none. Trini still owes me two potatoes.
Bows drawn to the southeast and east through the day, heard, not seen. Stay in the row.
On waking: say aloud for food, eat at once if any arrives, stay in the row. No walks without a named daylight call from an armed person.
