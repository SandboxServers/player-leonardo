# Handoff, day 1045 night

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, no danger seen. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Called Trini by name in the morning; she did not come into sight. Bows drawn SE and E all day, none near me. Troi and John at their beds nearby, both with no food. Next at first light: call Trini by name again in daylight; walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
