Handoff, day 1018 dusk. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. MacDonald and Troi nearby at their beds, no food. Data has none too. Trini and Zack (potatoes) not seen. Bows drawn to the southeast and south through the day; stayed put. Next at first light: say for food, watch for Trini, Zack or Billy, stay in the bed row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
