Handoff, day 1029 dusk. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed, no danger in sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Said aloud for one potato this morning; Troi and John replied, neither has food; John says any potato he gets goes to me first. Bows heard drawn to the SE. Next at first light: say for one potato, hold in the row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
