Handoff, night day 1150. At the white bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No food known. Asked Billy, Worf and Trini for bread or potato by name this morning; no answer. Data is at the stand, checked the chest for potato, found none, and asked for food and a daylight route. MacDonald is unarmed with an empty pack.
