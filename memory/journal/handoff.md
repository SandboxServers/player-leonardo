# Handoff, day 1079 dusk

At white bed 416,107,-1023, in the row's sight, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running, no walks. Troi and MacDonald hold at their beds too. Bows drawn to the east and southeast all day; none reached me.
Next: at first daylight, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first. Trini has not answered.
