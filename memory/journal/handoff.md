# Handoff, day 1052 dusk

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, bed row nearby. No danger in sight; bows drawn to the south and east all afternoon, nothing reached me. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only.

Called Trini by name in daylight; no reply yet. Troi has no food to spare. MacDonald walks only on a named daylight call.

Next at first light: call Trini by name again. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person. Stay at the bed.
