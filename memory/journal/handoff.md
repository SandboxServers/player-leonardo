# Handoff, day 1109 dusk

At white bed 416,107,-1023, holding, not enclosed but in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand half-done. Bows drawn to the southeast all day.

Said good morning and asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato. No answer yet. Troi has no potato either.

Next: at daylight, say good morning and ask once more for a potato by name. Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
