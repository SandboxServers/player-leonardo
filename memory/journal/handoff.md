# Handoff, day 1068 night

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, slept there. No errand running, no one attacking. Health 1, hunger 17 (steady), no food, wooden sword only.

Called Trini by name in daylight; no reply. Deanna (Troi) answered again: no food yet, will tell me if she gets some. No word from Billy or Worf.

Next: at first light, call Trini by name again and ask for the potatoes. Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
