# Handoff, day 918 (dusk)

At 416,107,-1023 beside the white bed in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Wooden sword in pack only.
Asked aloud at morning for a potato or bread. Macdonald, Data, Troi have none. Nobody in sight gave food.
Bows drawn to the south-east all day, no one hit.
On waking: ask in daylight, in sight, for food. Stay in the row. No walks in the dark. Owe Worf oak logs (ask first, do not promise).
