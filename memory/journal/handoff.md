# Handoff, day 1080 night

At white bed 416,107,-1023, in the row's sight, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running, no walks. Asked Trini by name for potatoes; Troi and MacDonald say I get the first potato. Bows drawn southeast and east through the day; none reached me.
Next: at first daylight, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first.
