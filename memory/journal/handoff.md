# Handoff, day 1052 night

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, bed row with Troi and MacDonald nearby. No danger seen, no errand running. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only.

Called Trini by name in daylight on day 1051 and again on 1052. She has not come. Troi has no food. MacDonald waits for a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the east and southeast through the day.

Next at first light: call Trini by name again. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person. Stay at bed.
