Handoff, day 1034 dusk. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed, with Troi and Data near. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Said aloud for food several times; Trini, Billy and Zack have not answered. Troi and MacDonald have none to share. Bows heard E and SE all day. Next at first light: wait for a potato from Trini or Billy; walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person. Pay Worf back for planks with oak logs when I have them.
