# Handoff, night of day 1058

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed (no blocks to wall in). Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Bows drawn to the south and southeast through the day; MacDonald sleeping beside the bed in the row.

Called Trini by name in daylight on day 1058 morning. No reply yet.

Next at first light: call Trini by name once more in daylight. Stay at the bed in others' sight. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person (Billy or Worf).
