# Handoff, night of day 1055

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Bow heard to the south. Slept once at the bed; going to bed again for the night.

Next at first light: call Trini by name in daylight, once. Stay at the bed in others' sight. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person (Billy or Worf).
