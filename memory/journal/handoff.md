Handoff, night day 1009. Asleep at white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Nobody in the row has food: MacDonald, Data, Troi all empty. Zack and Trini not seen today. Bows drawn to the south and east; stayed put all day. Next at first light: say aloud for food, watch for Trini or Zack, stay in the bed row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
