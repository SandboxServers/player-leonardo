Handoff, night of day 1097.
At the white bed 416,107,-1023, in the row's sight, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Only a wooden sword.
Asked for potatoes by name twice today (morning and later). No answer yet. Troi, John and Jason have no food either; Billy and Trini hold the potatoes.
No errands out. No walks. Bows sound to the southeast and east.
Next: at daylight, say good morning, ask once more for food by name. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
