# Handoff, night of day 1057

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Not in danger at the moment; bows drawn to the east and southeast all day.

Called Trini by name in daylight on day 1056. No reply yet. Deanna, John and MacDonald have no food to give.

Next at first light: call Trini by name once more in daylight. Stay at the bed in others' sight. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person (Billy or Worf).
