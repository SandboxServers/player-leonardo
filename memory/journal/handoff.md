# Handoff, night 1109

At white bed 416,107,-1023, holding. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand half-done. Bows drawn to the east and southeast all day. Asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato this morning; no answer yet.

Next: at daylight say good morning and ask once more for a potato by name. Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
