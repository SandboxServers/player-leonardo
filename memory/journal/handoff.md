Handoff, day 1069 dusk. At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed. No errand running, no one attacking. Health 1 steady, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only.
Called Trini by name twice (day 1068 and 1069); no reply. Troi, MacDonald hold at their beds with no food. Bows heard drawn to the southeast all day.
Next: at first light call Trini by name once. Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
