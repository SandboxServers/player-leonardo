Handoff, day 1036 dusk. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed, with MacDonald and Johnny near, all in sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Said aloud for food at dawn; Trini, Billy and Zack have not answered. Troi and MacDonald have none. Bows heard E and SE all day. Next at first light: say my need once more, wait for a potato. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person. Pay Worf back with oak logs when I have them.
