Handoff, day 1003 night. At bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed, nobody hostile in sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Morning: said aloud for food; MacDonald and Troi have none. Trini not seen; she holds my two potatoes. Bows drawn to the southeast and east all day; no walk taken.
