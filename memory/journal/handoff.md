# Handoff, day 1044 night (second)

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, not in danger at the moment. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Waited all day for Trini's potato; she has not come into sight. Troi holds nothing, John holds nothing. Bows drawn SE and E all day, none near me. Next at first light: wait for Trini by name in daylight; walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
