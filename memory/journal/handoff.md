Handoff, dusk of day 1006. At white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed, nobody in sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Next at first light: say aloud for food, watch for Trini or Zack, stay in the bed row, walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
