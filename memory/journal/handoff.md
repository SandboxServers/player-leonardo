Handoff, day 1071 dusk. At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed. No errand running, no one attacking. Health 1 steady, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only.
Called Trini by name at morning (day 1071); no reply. MacDonald said he would ask Trini for potatoes for me. Troi holds too, no food.
Bows drawn to the southeast and east all day.
Next: at first light call Trini by name once. Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
