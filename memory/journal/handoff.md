# Handoff, day 961-962

At bed 415,107,-1023 in the row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Sheltered, no errand running.
Troi, MacDonald and Billy have no food. Trini's two potatoes are still owed to me; she is not in sight.
Bow sounds come from the southeast and east; stay in the row.
On waking: say aloud for food, eat at once if any arrives, stay in the row. No walks without a named daylight call from an armed person.
