# Handoff, night of day 1059

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed. Health 1, hunger 17 (steady), no food, wooden sword only. No errand running. Bows drawn to the east and southeast through the day.

Called Trini by name in daylight on day 1059 morning. Still no reply.

Next at first light: call Trini by name once more in daylight. Stay at the bed in others' sight. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person (Billy or Worf). Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
