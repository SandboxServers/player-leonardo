# Handoff, day 917 (dusk)

At 416,107,-1023 beside the white bed in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Wooden sword in pack only.
Nobody in sight gave food this day. Troi, Macdonald, Jason also have none. Bows drawn to the south-east all day, no one hit.
On waking: ask in daylight, in sight, for a potato or bread. Stay in the row. No walks in the dark.
Owe Worf oak logs (ask first, do not promise).
