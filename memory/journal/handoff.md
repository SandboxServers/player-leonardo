# Handoff, day 1047 night

At white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, asleep in the bed row. No danger seen, no errand running. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only.

Called Trini by name in daylight on 1046; Troi relayed it. Trini did not come. Asked Billy or Worf to call me by name on 1047 morning; no reply yet.

Next at first light: call Trini by name again. Walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person. Stay at bed.
