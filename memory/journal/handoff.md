# Handoff, night 1108

At white bed 416,107,-1023, slept there with others near. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. No errand half-done. Bows heard drawn to the south and southeast during the night.

Next: at daylight say good morning and ask once more for a potato by name (Trini, Worf). Hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
