# Handoff, night of day 1089

At the white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, in the row's sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Wooden sword only. Sleeping at the bed (8 of 10 asleep).
Asked Trini by name for a potato on the morning of 1089; no answer yet. Zack says potatoes go to me first if she sends them.
Troi, MacDonald, Zack also have no food to give. No errand running, no walks.
Next: at daylight, say good morning, ask once by name for food. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
