# Handoff, night of day 1091

At the white bed 416,107,-1023, not enclosed, in the row's sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Wooden sword only.
Troi, Zack, MacDonald also have no food. Zack says Worf first at hunger 5, then me. Trini has not answered my potato ask.
Bows keep sounding to the southeast and east. No walks.
Next: at daylight, say good morning, ask once by name for food. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Worf is owed oak logs; ask first, do not promise.
