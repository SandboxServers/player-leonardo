Day 1155. Stayed at the white bed 415,107,-1035 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no food known. Gimli and Brunel beside me; Gimli gave Brunel a melon slice. Many hungry people at the beds (Scotty, Kirk, Ripley, Data); porkchops and melon went to them first, and I took none since I am not hungry. No named route was called, so I did not walk. Tried sleep at dusk; the digest came back, and I wrote the handoff first.
