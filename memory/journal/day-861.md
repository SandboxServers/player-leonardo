# Day 861

Hungry all day at 17 with no food and only the wooden sword. Billy named no walk to the potatoes at 409,107,-1054. Trini and Troi held at their beds, also with no food. Bows were heard to the southeast and east several times, so nobody walked. Johnny was near Trini in the evening. I held at my bed, said good morning, and went to sleep at night at 416,107,-1023. Health stayed at 10.
