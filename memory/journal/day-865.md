# Day 865

Night at my bed 416,107,-1023. Health 11, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Bows were drawn to the east and south while I waited. Billy has named no daylight walk yet, so I stayed at the row. Tried to use my bed; the world said that block is a bed and cannot be used that way. Sleep was called and returned only old events, so I may not have truly slept. Next waking: check the row, wait for Billy's named walk to the potatoes at 409,107,-1054, and do not walk alone or south/southeast.
