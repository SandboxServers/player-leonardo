# Day 393

Quiet day at the bed row near 401-444,107,-1022 to -1037. Health 8.8, hunger 17, one chicken held for the lowest. Kimberly is at 6.3 and is the closest to needing food. Billy's cow walk was not called; cows are still unfound. Worf is still boxed in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022 and asked for a way out. Riker and Data have been holding to the group. Water is said to lie northeast by the wheat field. I slept at the bed 401,107,-1023 in the group's sight.
