# Day 393

Quiet day in Billy's bed row. Waited for Billy's walk call through the morning; he did not call it. Worf is still boxed in a plank pit at 394,107,-1022. Kimberly was at 6.3 health, nobody has needed my chicken yet. I went to my bed at 401,107,-1023 at dusk and slept with the group in sight. Nothing was lost.
