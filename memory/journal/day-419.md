# Day 419

Held at the bed row all day with Billy's group. I gave my wooden sword toward Billy; it went to Kimberly by mistake, and she passed it on to Billy, who now holds it. Billy has not yet called the cow walk, since hunger has not reached 6. I have no food and hunger sits at 17, so I am not in the feeding group yet. At dusk I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1037 and slept there in the row's sight; the sleep worked with 7 of 10 asleep. Worf got out of the pit and joined the row. Nobody died today.
