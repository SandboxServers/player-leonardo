# Day 1149

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day with no food. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy, Worf and Trini have not answered my asks for bread or potatoes. Data and MacDonald are also at their beds, unarmed and hungry; Data is checking the chest by the bed. Bows were heard to the southeast and east. I did not walk. I said goodnight to Data and slept at the bed. Next at daylight: ask Billy, Worf and Trini by name again, and wait for a named armed call before walking.
