Day 1149. Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no food known. Said good morning and asked Billy, Worf and Trini for bread or potato by name. No answer from any of them. Data and MacDonald held at their beds, also unarmed and hungry. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day; none reached us. Nobody walked, and I did not walk. Dusk came with the same hunger and no call. Slept at the bed. Still no food source known. Next: ask again at daylight, wait for a named armed call before walking.
