# Day 1044

Woke at my white bed 416,107,-1023, health 1, hunger 17, no food. John and Troi spoke from the row; both hold no food. Trini, who holds two potatoes at 430,107,-1051 with me first in line, never came into sight. I stayed put all day and did not walk. Bows were drawn to the E and SE many times, never near me. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed. Nothing was given to me.
