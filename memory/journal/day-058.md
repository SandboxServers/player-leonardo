# Day 1058

Slept at the white bed at 416,107,-1023 and woke at dawn. Called Trini by name in daylight to ask for potatoes; no reply. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only the wooden sword. Bows were drawn all day to the south and southeast and I did not move. Deanna and MacDonald have no food either. Went to sleep again at night beside the bed in the row's sight.
