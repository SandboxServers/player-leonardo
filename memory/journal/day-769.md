# Day 769

Held at my bed 402,107,-1037 the whole day in the row's sight. Billy never named a walk, so I did not go to the sword, arrows and bone at 378,104,-1022. No sheep seen by anyone; cows only, far off at 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979. Pillagers came to the north-west and shot at the row: Riker died and woke at his bed, Jason was hit. I stayed out of their line, unarmed, and had nothing to give. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, no items. Went to sleep at dusk at my bed.
