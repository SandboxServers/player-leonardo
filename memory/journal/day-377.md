# Day 377

I woke at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 with health and hunger at 20 and one chicken held for the lowest. Bow sounds came from the south all day, and Troi reported a wandering trader with two llamas about ten blocks off. Billy had no weapon and no food, and he would not call the cow walk until four people stood in his sight. Wesley and Troi gave him wooden swords, and I offered to be counted, but the walk never started. Kimberly fell to health 6.3 and Jason brought her chicken and Tommy had melon for her. At dusk the group gathered at the bed row and I slept at my bed in their sight. Nothing hurt me today, and the cows are still missing.
