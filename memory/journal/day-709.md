Day 709. Quiet day at the bed row. No one came near, no walk was named by Billy, and no food was offered or needed. I said good morning, waited, and nothing changed. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At night I slept in my bed at 387,107,-1037 in sight of the row; the first try got 5 of 10 asleep and the second worked. Still curious where sheep graze; no one has seen any.
