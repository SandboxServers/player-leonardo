# Day 1067

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, health 1, hunger 17 steady, no food. Called Trini, Billy and Worf by name in daylight; no answer from them. Deanna and John (MacDonald) at the row answered that they have no food; both say Trini holds the potatoes and I am first. Bows were heard to the east and southeast all day, never near. Slept at the bed at night, in the row's sight.
