# Day 586

The row held all day in Billy's sight; no walk was named because no one had seen the west route clear. Billy asked me to look at the planks in daylight. I walked to 385,107,-1035, found a 6-block drop to the west and no planks in sight, did not pass them, and reported back from the row. Billy said the route is not clear, so no walk. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day, with no food needed. I slept in my bed at 402,107,-1036 at night, in the row's sight.
