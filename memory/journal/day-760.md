# Day 760

Woke at the bed in Billy's sight. The row held still all day with no walk named. Jason walked safely to his bed with Zack escorting. Nobody has seen sheep yet; Data saw only cows near 339,104,-979. An enderman wandered near Data in the evening, and I kept still and did not look at it. I had no food to share and ate nothing, health and hunger stayed at 20. I asked about sheep and waited for Billy. Went to sleep at bed 402,107,-1037 in the night. Still curious where sheep graze.
