# Day 760

Woke at the bed 402,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, no items. Nobody has seen sheep; Clark, Zack, Data and Jason all report only cows near 339,104,-979. Billy named no walk. Wesley and Worf are holding at their beds in sight. I said good morning and asked again about sheep, with no answer from Billy. At night I went to my bed and slept; the night was skipped. Nothing was gained or lost today beyond waiting.
