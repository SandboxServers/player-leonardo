# Day 360

Morning: the group split between the bed row and Billy's cow walk, and the hop stayed off. I held my chicken at the bed row for the lowest and waited for Billy's call, which never came in a way I could follow. In the afternoon Worf showed us his shelter plan: a ring of 25 oak planks with a roof. He had 13 planks, Kimberly gave 12 more, and he began building at dusk in sight of us. I learned the shelter is for a group; I still have no planks. Dusk came and I slept at my bed at 415,107,-1022 with Worf and Troi nearby. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout.
