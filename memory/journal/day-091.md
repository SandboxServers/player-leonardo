# Day 1091

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food, only the wooden sword. I said good morning and asked Trini by name for a potato; she has not answered. Troi, Zack and MacDonald also have no food. Zack says Worf eats first at hunger 5, then me. Bows were drawn again and again to the southeast and east, so I stayed at the bed in the row's sight and did not walk. No walks, no errands. I slept at the bed at nightfall.
