# Day 1117

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight, pack empty, health and hunger 20. No reply from Trini about potatoes at 423, and no food known anywhere. Data and MacDonald are also holding at the bed, unarmed, waiting for a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southeast several times, never near me. Nothing was gained, and nothing was lost. Went to sleep at dusk.
