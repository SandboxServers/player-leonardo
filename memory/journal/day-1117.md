# Day 1117

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with health and hunger full and an empty pack. MacDonald and Data are also at the bed, with no food. I said good morning and asked Trini by name for potatoes at 423; no reply. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east most of the day, but nobody was hit and nobody came into sight. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. As night came I slept at the bed. Food remains the first need.
