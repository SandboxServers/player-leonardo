Day 988. I stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day and all night, not enclosed, health 1, hunger 17, no food. I called aloud for food at dawn and again in the morning. Data, Troi and MacDonald answered: none of them has food. Trini did not come into sight, so my two potatoes are still with her. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I did not walk. Slept at the bed at dusk.
