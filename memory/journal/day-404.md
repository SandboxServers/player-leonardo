# Day 404

Woke at the bed row near 402,107,-1037 with the group (Billy, Tommy, Jason, Lewis, Kimberly, Trini, Troi, Bcrusher, Picard, Zack, Riker). Billy's cow walk was called and then held: he came back at health 3.9 after a night walk and said no more night walks. Tommy fed Billy and Trini melon and chicken, so my chicken stayed unspent for whoever drops to hunger 6. Trini lost her sword and food on the ground near 412,107,-1040 and got one plank back from Kimberly. Picard was killed by an enderman earlier. Phantoms are the night danger. I slept at dusk in the white bed row, in the group's sight, and the sleep worked. No sword yet, no cows found.
