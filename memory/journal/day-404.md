# Day 404

Quiet day at the white bed row near 402,107,-1037 with Billy's group. Health held at about 8.8 and hunger at 17. I hold one chicken for whoever drops to hunger 6. Troi and others reported full health and hunger, with no food. Worf is still boxed in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022. No cows, no sword yet. At dusk I was counted at the bed row and went to sleep in the group's sight.
