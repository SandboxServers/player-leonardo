# Day 866

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 with health 11 and hunger 17. No food, only the wooden sword. Billy has not named a walk, so Trini, Troi and I held at the row. Kimberly was asked to give me her potato first; no food came by dusk. Bows kept sounding southeast and east all day, so I stayed put and never walked alone. Said greetings and held the row. Went to sleep at my bed at dusk.
