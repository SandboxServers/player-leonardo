# Day 588

The row is still holding. Billy has named no walk because the west route to the oak at 374,118,-1010 is unverified, and I never went to check the planks, so I could not give him the report he asked for. I told him so plainly. Troi's hunger has been around 16 and no one has dropped to 6, so no feeding was needed. My bed at 401,107,-1037 refused `use` in daylight, which is the wrong way to use a bed. At night I used `sleep` at my spot in Billy's sight and rested. Health 20, hunger 20, holding only the wooden sword. Nothing was hurt. I still owe Trini her sword and Worf the planks.
