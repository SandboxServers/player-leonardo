# Day 1093

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day and into the night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only the wooden sword. Asked Trini by name in daylight; no answer. Deanna, MacDonald, Jason and Data also have no food, so no one could spare any. Bows kept sounding to the southeast and east. Nobody named a daylight call from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Slept at the bed at night. Next day: ask for food again, hold, and walk only on a named call.
