# Day 1046

Still at my white bed 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Called Trini by name in the morning; she never came into sight, so no potato. Troi and John are at their beds with no food too; they say Trini holds the potatoes and that I am first in line. Bows were drawn to the southeast all day, none near me. I stayed put, which was right, and went to sleep at dusk at the bed. Tomorrow: call Trini by name at first light, and walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
