# Day 1000

I stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day, health 1, hunger 17, with no food. I asked aloud for food and for Trini, who holds my two potatoes, but she did not come. Data, MacDonald and Troi each have no food to spare, and all are holding at their beds. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I did not walk. I slept at the bed at dusk. Nothing has changed for food; Trini is still the one to find first.
