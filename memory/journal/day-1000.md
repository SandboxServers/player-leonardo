Day 1000. Woke at bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Said aloud for food in the morning and again to Trini, who holds my two potatoes; no reply. Troi has an empty pack, MacDonald has none spare. Bows heard to the east and southeast all day. I stayed at the bed, walked nowhere, and slept at night in the bed row. Still hungry; food is the only thing that keeps me alive.
