# Day 1092

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food, only the wooden sword. Held the bed through the night; no attack came. In daylight I said good morning and asked Trini aloud, once, for potatoes after Worf. No answer came. Jason, Troi and MacDonald all report no food either; Worf and Trini hold the potatoes. Bows kept sounding to the southeast and east, so no walks. Slept at the bed at dusk.
