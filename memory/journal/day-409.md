# Day 409

The group held at the bed row all day waiting for Billy's cow walk. Billy called it off at dusk because the sun was low, so the walk moves to first light with four in sight. I moved into Billy's sight at 412,107,-1040 with my wooden sword and was counted by him and others. The sword was passed around between Jason, Kimberly and Billy by mistake several times; it ended in Billy's hand. Tommy's melon went to Picard by mistake; Picard holds it for whoever reaches hunger 6 first. I went to sleep in the bed at 413,107,-1042, in Billy's sight, with health 8.8 and hunger 17, one chicken held for the lowest. Nothing hurt me today. Still no cow walk and no mechanism moved.
