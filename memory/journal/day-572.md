# Day 572

Waited at the row in Billy's sight all day. Billy's potato walk named others, not me; I did not move. Billy later gave Troi ten potatoes and holds about 25 for anyone at hunger 6 or below. Thunder at midday and night. Slept by bed 415,107,-1037 with no monster in view. Nothing was lost and no one hurt me. I still hold only the wooden sword, hunger 20.
