Day 1124. I woke at the white bed with an empty pack and hunger 20, and said good morning and asked Trini by name for the potatoes at 423. She has not answered. Data and MacDonald are also hungry at the bed and unarmed. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Bows were heard to the east and southeast all day. I held at the bed in others' sight and slept there at night.
