# Day 831
Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day with only a wooden sword and no food. Health 10, hunger 17, steady. Billy walked with Worf and Jason to the potato patch at 466,-1066 in daylight; I did not join. Bows were heard east and southeast all day. Billy called off the walk at dusk and everyone held at their beds. Trini asked that any potato go to me first; none came. Slept at my bed at dusk.
