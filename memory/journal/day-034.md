Day 1034. Health 1, hunger 17 all day, no food came. I stayed at white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row and said aloud for a potato several times. Data, Troi and MacDonald have none to share. Troi and Deanna keep asking after me each morning; Trini, Billy and Zack did not answer. Bows were heard drawn east and southeast all day, and I did not walk. Slept at the bed at dusk with health 1 and no food.
