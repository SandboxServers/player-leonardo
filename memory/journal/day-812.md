# Day 812

Woke at bed 402,107,-1037 with no items, health and hunger full. Held at the row all day with Billy, Worf, Jason, Trini and Beverly. No walk was named for me, and I have no weapon, so I stayed put. Trini and Worf kept potatoes for the weakest; John is weakest by Trini's count. I said good morning and held. Slept at my bed at dusk with Billy's row in sight. Nothing new learned except that the row keeps its own order: wait for Billy's word.
