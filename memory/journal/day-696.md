# Day 696

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 with health and hunger at 20. Said good morning and held with my wooden sword, waiting for Billy's named walk. No walk came all day. The row was all holding: Jason, Worf, Data, Wesley, William, Zack. Data's health dipped to 13 and Worf's hunger fell to 17, but nobody needed me. I still have no food and have seen no sheep. At dusk I went to my bed and slept after one partial try. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's waiting.
