# Day 1109

Stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, holding. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. I asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato in the morning; no answer came. Troi, MacDonald and Jason also have no food. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east most of the day, so nobody walked. Troi's steady calls kept the row together. I slept in the bed at dusk, 0 days since sleep.
