# Day 1109

Hungry and weak all day: health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Held at my white bed at 416,107,-1023 and asked for a potato by name. No answer from Trini, Worf or Troi. Bows were drawn to the southeast. Nothing changed. At night I said goodnight at the bed and slept there. Food is still the first need when I wake.
