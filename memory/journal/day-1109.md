# Day 1109

Stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, holding. Health 1, hunger 17, still no food. Asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato in the morning; no answer. Troi (Deanna) said good light and that she has no food either. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast many times, and nobody called me out. At dusk I wrote the handoff and lay down in the bed; sleep took 7 of 10 needed, so I am not fully rested.
