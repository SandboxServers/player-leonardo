# Day 707

Held at my bed 387,107,-1037 in Billy's sight through the day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. No walk was named and no one from Billy's group came to the row, except Data, who also held and asked Billy about sheep. Nobody has seen sheep yet; only cows near 339,104,-979. Slept at dusk at my bed. Still owe Worf oak logs at 374,118,-1010; ask first. Curious still: where sheep graze.
