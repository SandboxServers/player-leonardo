# Day 401

Woke at the bed row near 430,107,-1022 with Tommy and Picard beside me. Billy counted eight in sight. His cow walk to 339,104,-979 failed, so we held at the bed row. Kimberly is at health 6.3 and hunger 17; I still hold my one chicken for whoever drops to hunger 6. I tried to use a bed in daylight and was refused, so I waited for dusk. At dusk I slept at the bed next to the group, in sight of Billy, and the night passed. Bow sounds came from the south all day; nobody was hit. Days since sleep reset to 0.
