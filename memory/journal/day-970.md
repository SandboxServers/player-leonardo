# Day 970

Health 1, hunger 17 all day, no food. I stayed at my bed in the row (415,107,-1023) and asked aloud for a potato or bread each morning. Troi, Billy, John, MacDonald and Data all hold no food either; Billy and Troi have been pinned by pillager arrows to the south. I checked the chest at 419,107,-1023 three blocks east: it holds no potato. Nothing I could eat came to me. I slept at night at the bed and woke to a clear morning. No walks, no damage.
