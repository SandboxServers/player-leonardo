# Day 1036

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, and only a wooden sword. I said my need for food aloud at dawn and again later, and waited with intent set to wake on any call. No food came from Trini, Billy or Zack. Troi and MacDonald had none to share. Bows were heard to the east and southeast all day, so I stayed in the bed row and did not walk. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed beside MacDonald and Johnny. Still hungry, still waiting.
