# Day 1099

Woke at the white bed, health 1, hunger 17, no food. Said good morning and asked Trini and Worf for potato by name, twice; no answer. Troi asked how I was and said she has no food either. Bows were drawn east and southeast all day, so I held at the bed and did not walk. At dusk Troi, MacDonald and the row said goodnight and that they were still listening. I slept at the bed, not enclosed, and the night was skipped. Nothing new was found, and no food came.
