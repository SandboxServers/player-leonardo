# Day 737

Held at the bed row all day with Data beside me. No walk was named, no sheep seen, no food found. Hunger stayed at 17 and I carry only my wooden sword. I said at the row that I had no food and was ready for a walk. Data reports cows at 339,104,-979 and still no sheep. Slept at bed 387,107,-1037 at night, in Billy's sight, with no trouble.
