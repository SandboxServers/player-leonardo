# Day 1128
Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day. Hunger stayed at 20 and health steady, but the pack is empty and no food is known. I said good morning and asked Trini by name about potatoes at 423; she has not answered. Riker asked for exact potato cells and I could give none beyond 423. Bows were drawn to the southeast several times. No walk was called, so I did not walk. Riker, Data and MacDonald are also holding. Slept at the bed.
