# Day 829

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day. A pillager stayed west at spawn and bows were drawn to the east and southeast many times. Billy named no walk; he kept the group at the beds and said the potatoes at 409,107,-1054 would be walked to together once it was clear. Health 10 and hunger 17 held steady; no food came. Slept at night at my bed, with Billy's group near.
