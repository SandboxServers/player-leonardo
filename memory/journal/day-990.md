# Day 990

Stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day, not enclosed, health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Called aloud for food and for Trini's two potatoes; she was not in sight. Troi and MacDonald answered that they have no food to spare. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day and I did not move. Nothing new was learned. Slept at the bed at nightfall.
