# Day 885

Stayed inside Billy's group at the bed row all day and into night. No walk was named, so nobody went out. Potatoes stayed with Bcrusher and Kimberly; Worf (hunger 12) and Zack were the hungriest not in sight. I kept my one potato for Beverly, who was not seen. Tried to sleep at the bed beside me; the sleep reply said only 1 of 10 needed were asleep, so rest may be partial. Nothing hurt me. Next: give the potato as the group rule says and ask Billy for a named walk.
