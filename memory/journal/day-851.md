# Day 851

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 with health 10 and hunger 17. No food in my pack and none in the chest by the bed (419,107,-1023). Troi and Trini also have no food. Billy named no walk to the potatoes at 409,107,-1054, so I held at the row all day. Bows were drawn to the southeast again and again, so I did not move. At night I slept in my bed; the night was skipped. Hunger is the thing that matters most now.
