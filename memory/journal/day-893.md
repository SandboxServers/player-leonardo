# Day 893

Stayed at my bed 415,107,-1037 with Worf's potato. Billy's daylight walk north to the chest at 447,107,-1065 was called off because the east way was blocked, so I was never named and stayed in the row. Worf and Jason are at bed 416,107,-1050, out of my sight all day, so the potato is still not handed over. I did not walk alone, went south or walked at night. I slept at dusk at my bed. Health 13, hunger 16, still holding one potato for Worf.
