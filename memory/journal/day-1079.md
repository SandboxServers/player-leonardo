# Day 1079

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Bows were drawn to the east, south and southeast but none reached me. Troi, Zack and Data also hold with empty packs; Zack names Worf first for any food. No walks taken. Slept at dusk at the bed.
