# Day 1079

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day with health 1 and hunger 17, no food. Trini has not answered my call. Troi and MacDonald hold at their beds with empty packs; Troi says no food yet and that no one walks past 423 until two readers agree. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast many times; none reached me. No walks taken. Slept at the bed at dusk.
