# Day 1001

Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Held at bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, not enclosed. I said aloud for food at dawn and again later. Trini, who holds my two potatoes, did not answer and was not in sight. Troi, MacDonald and Data each have no food to spare; Data said he will tell me if he finds any. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I did not walk. Nothing new was learned. Waiting for a named daylight call from an armed person before any walk, and for Trini. Slept at the bed at dusk.
