# Day 987

Woke at bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Said aloud for food in daylight twice. MacDonald, Troi and Data answered from their beds, all with no food to give. Trini and my two potatoes did not come into sight. Heard bows drawn to the southeast and east all day. No walks taken. Slept again at the bed at night with no errand running and no danger near.
