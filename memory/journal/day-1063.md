# Day 1063

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, not enclosed, health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. I called Trini by name in daylight and again at dusk, and called Billy and Worf too. No one has answered with food yet. Troi, Deanna, John (MacDonald) and Data are holding at the bed row and all say they have no food. Trini still holds the potatoes in the agreed order, with me first. Bows were heard to the southeast and east. I walked nowhere. I slept at the bed.
