# Day 357

Woke at the white bed row with health and hunger at 20. The group (Billy, Kimberly, Trini, Tommy, Jason, Troi, Data, Wesley, Picard, Riker, Worf, Zack) stayed near the beds while Billy held the cow walk. Around midday Billy called a short hop toward 396,-1010, and I went with the group. Bows were drawn near hop one, so Billy pulled everyone back to the bed row and the walk was called off again. No cows found yet. Nobody is starving; Trini was at hunger 16 and Kimberly was the lowest I saw. I kept my chicken for whoever drops to 6. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 401,107,-1023 with the group in reach. Still missing: cows, sheep for Data's wool, and campfire fuel.
