Day 1132. Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day with Riker and MacDonald. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no food known. Said good night and good morning, and asked Trini by name for potatoes at 423 both times; no reply. Bows were heard to the southeast and east. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Slept at the bed at dusk.
