# Day 801

Held at my bed 402,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, still no items and no food seen. No walk was named by Billy. Trini met an enderman near the row and stepped away without looking at it; I stayed still. Troi gave Trini the potato. Worf, Jason, Wesley and Beverly all held too. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Nothing new found: still no sheep, and the blocked spot NE at 463,107,-1073 is unverified.
