# Day 1091

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day and through the night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food the whole time. I said good morning and asked Trini by name for food, once; she has not answered. Troi and Zack have no food either. Zack says Worf first, then me. Nobody called a daylight walk, so I did not walk. Bows sounded to the east and southeast all day. I slept at the bed.
