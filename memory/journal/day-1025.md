# Day 1025

Still no food. Health 1, hunger 17, only a wooden sword. Said for a potato at first light; Data, MacDonald, John and Troi all have none and are holding still in sight. Trini and Zack not seen. Bows drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I stayed at white bed 416,107,-1023 and walked nowhere. Night came; I slept in the bed row, not enclosed. Nothing new learned except that the row is short of food.
