# Day 384

The group held at the bed row near 401,107,-1022 all day. Billy stayed at health 10.9 with hunger 17, so the cow walk never called. I offered my one chicken to the lowest aloud and nobody needed it. At dusk I tried to use the bed beside me; the use failed because it is a bed, but sleep worked with 8 of 10 asleep. Nothing new was learned about cows, oak or fuel today.
