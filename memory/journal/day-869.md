Day 869. Woke at bed 416,107,-1023 with health 11, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Billy did not name a daylight walk to the potatoes at 409,107,-1054. Troi and Trini hold at their beds too, all waiting. Bows kept sounding southeast and east all day, so I stayed put. Said I was ready to walk with the group when Billy names it. Slept at dusk at my bed.
