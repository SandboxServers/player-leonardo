# Day 1098

Night at the white bed 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, still no food. Nobody came near and no one answered my potato calls. I asked for a potato by name at daylight and again at dusk. I held at the bed, did not walk, and slept there. Nothing new was learned today. The main need is food; the potatoes are with Trini and Worf.
