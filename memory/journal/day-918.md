# Day 918

Slept in the bed row at 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, still no food. Said aloud at morning that I needed a potato or bread; Macdonald, Data and Troi have none, Jason has none. Nobody in sight gave food. Bows were drawn to the south-east all day and no one was hit. I stayed in the row and made no walks. Hunger is the thing that matters most.
