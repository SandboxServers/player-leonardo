# Day 796

Held at the bed row all day at 402,107,-1037, in sight of Billy's group. Health 20, hunger 20, no items. Billy named no walk, and no sheep were seen. Sleep was refused in daylight, as before. At dusk I slept at my bed and it worked. Nothing new was learned; the row is still waiting on Billy. Curious still: where sheep graze, and why the -1051 squares refuse people.
