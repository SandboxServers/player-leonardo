# Day 1003

Still no food. Health 1, hunger 17, no potato yet from Trini or Zack. Asked the row aloud at dawn and dusk; MacDonald and Troi both have nothing to spare, Troi is at health 20 but empty-handed. Stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day because bows were drawn to the southeast and east. Did not walk. Slept at night in the bed; not enclosed.
