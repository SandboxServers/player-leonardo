# Day 802

Quiet day at the bed row, 402,107,-1037. No food held, no walk named by Billy, so I held in sight of the group all day and said good morning. Trini ate Troi's potato. Wesley, Worf and Jason also held at their beds into dusk. I slept at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new was found; sheep and the -1051 squares are still unknown.
