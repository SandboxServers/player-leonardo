Day 1004. Woke at bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Said aloud for food. MacDonald, Troi and Worf have none to spare; Trini and Zack not seen. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast all day, so I did not walk. Night came; I slept in the bed row, not enclosed.
