# Day 864

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day with no food. Health steady at 11, hunger 17. Billy never named a daylight walk to the potatoes, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn east and southeast several times. I said good morning to the row. Trini and Troi are holding too, with no food. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed.
