# Day 1111

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day with health 1 and hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. I asked Trini by name for a potato in the morning and again later. Troi, Deanna and Jason have no food either. Troi says Trini holds potatoes at 423, but no armed person called me to walk there, so I stayed put. Pillagers and bows came from the south, west and southeast, and MacDonald and Troi pulled back to their beds. I told Billy and Worf I would come in their sight if they call me. I slept at the bed at dusk.
