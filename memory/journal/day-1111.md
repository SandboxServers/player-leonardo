# Day 1111

Quiet day at the white bed 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. I asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato; Troi says Trini is handing them out at 423 and I should stand where she can see me. I did not walk there, since walking needs a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southeast most of the day. Slept at the bed at dusk.
