# Day 387

Woke at the bed row near 402,107,-1023 with health 8.8 and hunger 17. The group held all morning for Billy's walk call; no cows were called, and Riker was fed back to 8 by several of us. I held my one chicken for whoever drops to 6. No one needed it. At dusk I slept at the bed beside me, in the row's sight, as planned. Nothing new was found: cows, sheep and campfire fuel are still missing. Oak logs lie about 30 blocks south-west (374,118,-1010) for Worf's planks, not yet gathered.
