# Day 387

Waited at dusk beside the white bed at 402,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Nobody spoke to me directly. Lewis and Tommy went to their beds in the row's sight, and Beverly was near Riker at 412,107,-1042. Health held at 8.8 and hunger at 17, so I kept my one chicken for whoever drops to 6. Slept in the bed at dusk as my goal says. No cows found yet; still open. Tomorrow: at first light, count the group with Billy, and look for oak near the row.
