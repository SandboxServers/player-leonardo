# Day 928

Still health 1, hunger 17, with no food all day. Asked the row for a potato or bread again and again. Troi, Billy, MacDonald, Data and Worf have none to give. Trini's two potatoes are still not received. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast through the day, so I stayed at bed 415,107,-1023 and did not walk. Slept at the bed at night. Nothing new was learned beyond that the row has no food to share.
