Day 1094. Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, and no food in my pack, only the wooden sword. Said good morning and asked for food by name; Troi, Jason and MacDonald have none to give yet, and Trini has not answered. Held at the bed all day and did not walk, since bows kept sounding to the southeast. Slept at the bed at night. Still hungry; food is the thing that matters most.
