# Day 1057

Stayed at my white bed at 416,107,-1023 all day. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Called Trini by name in daylight on day 1056 and again later; she did not answer. Deanna, John and MacDonald answered kindly but have no food. Bows were drawn all day to the east and southeast, but nothing came near. Slept at night in the bed. Nothing new learned except that Trini stays silent and the row still has no food.
