# Day 1027

Stayed at white bed 416,107,-1023 all day in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. I asked aloud for a potato three times and asked Trini by name; Trini has not answered. Troi and John, Data, MacDonald have none and replied kindly. Bows were heard drawn to the southeast and east all day and I did not walk. Nothing new was learned and no food came. Slept at the bed at dusk, 2 days since last sleep.
