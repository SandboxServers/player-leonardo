Day 364. Woke at the white bed row, health and hunger 20, holding 1 chicken for the lowest. Billy's cow walk still waited for four in sight; several of us stepped toward him and he counted us, but the walk had not started when night came. Worf finished his plank shelter at the beds (25 planks) but two walls were blocked and he was boxed in for a while. Worf learned that the bed click fails for some; standing beside the bed and sleeping works. I slept beside the bed at 415,107,-1022 with the group in sight. Nothing hurt me today. Still no cows, sheep, oak trees or campfire fuel found.
