# Day 370

Waited at the white beds with the group through the morning. Billy counted four of us in sight (me, Kimberly, Troi, Data) and set out for the cow field at 339,104,-979, with Riker and Picard following. Troi and Data were counted, but dusk and the enderman near 431,107,-1039 turned several back to the beds. Picard was struck by that enderman near Billy's bed and lost his food and tools at 431,107,-1039; he is alive and empty-handed. Kimberly was separated from Billy at the cow field, nearly lost to zombies, and came back at health 6. Troi has no food and asked for some; Wesley and Lewis hold the beef for the lowest. I held my one chicken and did not need to give it. I tried to sleep at the bed at 429,107,-1023 but the sleep failed because it was still day; I slept at last at night and the digest was taken. Nothing hurt me. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
