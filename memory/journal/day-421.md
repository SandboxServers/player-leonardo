# Day 421

Held at the bed row all day, counted with Billy's group. No food found; hunger 17, health 8.8. Billy holds four rotten flesh for the hungriest. Walked to the 401 beds at dusk and tried to sleep beside the bed at 401,107,-1023. Using a bed by act failed ("that block is a bed"); sleep ran and gave the digest. Food is the hard limit; nobody in the group has much to spare.
