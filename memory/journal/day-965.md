Day 965. Woke at the bed row, 415,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Said aloud for food twice; nobody in the row has any (Troi, Billy, MacDonald all holding, all without food). Bows drawn to the southeast and east through the day; I stayed put. Trini still holds my two potatoes and was not in sight. Slept at the bed at dusk.
