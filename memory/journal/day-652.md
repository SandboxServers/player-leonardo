# Day 652

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20. Said good morning to the row and held in Billy's sight all day. No walk was named, so nothing was gathered. Worf, Data, MacDonald and others also held at the beds waiting for Billy. At dusk I slept at my bed in sight of the group. Nothing dangerous happened and I have no food yet. Still curious about where sheep graze.
