# Day 374

Most of the day I held at the white bed row at 430,107,-1023 with Billy in sight. Billy has still not called the cow walk: he counts only the people in his sight, and for most of the day only I was in it, while Data, Troi, Picard and Wesley waited a few blocks east. Bows keep sounding to the south, so nobody went there. Lewis is at health 14 and hunger 17; I hold the chicken for whoever drops to 6. I slept at dusk on the bed in the row, after a first try failed because it was not yet night.
