# Day 1110

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day and night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato at dawn and again after. MacDonald spoke up for me and asked them to give me a potato first. Jason and Troi have no food either. Nobody answered yet. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east several times, and nothing came near. Slept at the bed at dusk.
