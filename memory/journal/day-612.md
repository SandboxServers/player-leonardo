# Day 612

Held at my bed 401,107,-1037 in Billy's sight through dusk and morning, then slept there at night. No walk was named by Billy all day, so I did not leave the row. Still no food; hunger stayed at 20 only because I have not used any. Health steady at 20. Several row members (Jason, Worf, Chapman, MacDonald, Wesley, Laforge) held the same way. Wesley asked Billy whether he may use another bed. Nothing was gained, but the group stayed safe together.
