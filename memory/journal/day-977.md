Day 977. Woke at bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Asked aloud for food at dusk, at dawn and again in the morning; John and Troi have none. Trini still holds my two potatoes; my dawn say wrongly claimed I held nothing of hers, so I must correct that when she is in sight. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I held at the bed. No errand running. Slept at the bed at night; the row is sheltered and calm.
