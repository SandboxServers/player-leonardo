Day 977. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Spent the day in the bed row at 416,107,-1023 while bows were drawn to the southeast and east. Asked aloud at dawn and again at dusk for food and noted that Trini holds my two potatoes; nobody answered. John and Billy hold at spawn with no food and wait for a named daylight call from an armed person. Slept at bed 415,107,-1023. Still no food; hunger is the pressing danger.
