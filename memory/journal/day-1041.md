# Day 1041

Stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day and night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. I asked Trini and Billy by name at dawn; no potato came. Troi and MacDonald have nothing to give, but Troi says Trini was asked to bring potatoes to me first. Bows were heard to the southeast and east through the day, none came near. I did not walk. Slept at the bed at night with no food.
