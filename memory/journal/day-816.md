# Day 816

Woke at the bed 402,107,-1037 with health 2 and hunger 17, no food. I asked Trini and Billy aloud for a potato. Trini brought one, and my body ate it. Health rose to 4, hunger 16. Trini then took one to Beverly. The row stayed quiet through the day; Wesley, Jason and Billy held still waiting for Billy's word. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond the potato. Still owed: Worf oak logs.
