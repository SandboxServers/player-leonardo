# Day 862

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 at health 10, hunger 17, with no food, only a wooden sword. Said good morning and asked Billy for the potato walk. Billy gave his ten potatoes to Worf (5) and Zack (3) and did not name a walk for me. Trini, Troi and Data hold at their beds, all with no food. Bows kept sounding southeast and east all day, so I did not leave the bed. Hunger is the limit; I will keep asking for a daylight walk.
