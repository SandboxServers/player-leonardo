Day 999. Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Asked aloud for food at first light; Troi and MacDonald have none spare. Trini (holds my two potatoes) never came into sight. Bows drawn to the east and southeast all day, so I stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 and did not walk. Went to sleep at dusk in the bed row, not enclosed, nobody hostile in sight.
