# Day 1020

Still health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Slept at white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, in sight of Troi and John. Said aloud for food to Trini, Zack and Billy; none came. Troi, John, Data and MacDonald have no food either but stay in sight. Bows were drawn to the south and east during the day; I stayed put. No walks taken.
