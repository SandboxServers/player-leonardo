Day 1123. Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning and asked Trini by name for potatoes at 423; no answer. Billy and Worf did not call, so I stayed at the bed. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east through the day. MacDonald and Data held at the bed too, both without food or arms. Nothing was gathered and nothing walked. Slept at the bed at night; days since sleep reset.
