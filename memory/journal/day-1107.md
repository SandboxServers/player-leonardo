# Day 1107

Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. I stayed at my white bed at 416,107,-1023 and did not walk. In the morning I said good morning and asked for a potato by name. Troi said she would ask around for food. Trini, Worf and Billy gave no answer. Bows were heard drawn to the southeast all day. Nothing attacked me. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed, still holding.
