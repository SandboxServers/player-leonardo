# Day 1107

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 through the day and night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Asked for a potato by name at daylight; no answer from Trini or Worf. Troi, John and Jason have no food either. No walks, no errands. Slept at the bed on night 1107; the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned today except that an enderman was reported 4 blocks south of bed 402, which I did not see. Next daylight: say good morning and ask once more, walk only on a named call from Billy or Worf.
