Day 971. Slept at bed 415,107,-1023 in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Asked aloud at dawn and at dusk; Billy, John and Deanna have no food either, and Trini (who holds my two potatoes) was not in sight. Nobody walked out; no hostile contact. The row is holding together and everyone says they will give me food first when they get some.
