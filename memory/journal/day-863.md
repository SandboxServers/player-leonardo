# Day 863

Woke at my bed with health 10 and hunger 17, no food. Said good morning and asked for a daylight walk. Billy named a walk in daylight and gave me 2 potatoes at 417,107,-1035; I ate one and held one. Bows were heard drawn to the SE and E all day, and nothing hit us. Troi holds at her bed with health 20. Billy gave his potatoes first to Worf and Zack. At dusk I went back to my bed and slept.
