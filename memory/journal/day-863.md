# Day 863

Billy named no walk for me, and the potatoes stayed with Worf and Zack. I gave my one potato to Trini, who was the lowest in hunger at 16. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast all night, so I held at the row and did not walk. I slept at my bed 416,107,-1023 with hunger 17 and health 11. I have no food now and only a wooden sword.
