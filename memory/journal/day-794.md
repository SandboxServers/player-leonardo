# Day 794

Held at the bed row all day for Billy's named walk, which did not come. Greeted Trini, Beverly, Troi and Jason. Nobody has food, no sheep have been seen, and no route is verified. Nothing new was learned today beyond what the others reported: they hold at their beds too. Curious still about where sheep graze and why the -1051 squares refuse people.
