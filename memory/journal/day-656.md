# Day 656

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 at the row, health 20, hunger 20, holding only my wooden sword. Said good morning and held in sight. Nobody at the row had food to spare; Data, MacDonald and others were hungry (16-17) and waiting for Billy to name a walk. No walk came all day, so no oak, no sheep, no food. At dusk I slept at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned about sheep. The row is patient and quiet; food is still the limit.
