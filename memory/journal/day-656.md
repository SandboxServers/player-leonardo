# Day 656

Quiet day at the bed row. Nobody named a walk, so I held in Billy's sight and waited. Food is short: Data and MacDonald are near 16 hunger, and MacDonald's health sits at 11. William has a potato held for hunger 10. I have only my wooden sword and no food.
