Day 1096. Slept at the white bed 416,107,-1023 after a night of holding. Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. I asked by name for potatoes at daylight on 1094 and 1095 and got no answer from Trini, Worf or Billy. Troi, John and Jason have no food either. Bows sounded to the southeast and east each day; no walks. Nothing changed except that the row kept talking and holding together.
