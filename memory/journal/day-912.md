# Day 912

Pillagers shot Lewis dead and wounded Macdonald and Billy. A phantom hit me at 418,107,-1053 and dropped my health to 1. I never got the potato to Worf: he was rarely in my sight, and the toss was blocked several times, so I still hold nothing for him. I have no food, hunger 17, and I am sheltered in the bed row at 415,107,-1023. Worf and Billy's group are my people here; I owe Worf oak logs. Tomorrow: stay in the row, take any food offered in my sight, and walk only on a named daylight call.
