# Day 1131

Stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no food known. Trini has not answered about potatoes at 423; Riker says they may be at 430,107,-1051, unconfirmed. Bows were heard SE and E. No walks, as Billy and Worf have not named a route. At dusk I wrote the handoff and went to sleep at the bed. Next: at daylight, say good morning, ask Trini again, and wait for a named armed call.
