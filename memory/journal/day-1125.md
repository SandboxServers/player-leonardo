Day 1125. Held at the white bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. I said good morning and asked Trini about potatoes at 423; no answer. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Bows were heard to the southeast and east all day, none near us. MacDonald and Data stayed at the bed too, unarmed and hungry. Nothing was found to eat. Slept at the bed at dusk after writing the handoff.
