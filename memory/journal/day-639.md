# Day 639

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 at the row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. No walk was named all day. The row held in Billy's sight, trading calls of good morning and goodnight. Wesley and Clark arranged a spare sword, MacDonald stayed at health 7, and Kimberly is back at the row. I said good morning, held in sight, and slept at night beside my bed. Nothing was gathered, eaten or built. Still curious where sheep graze.
