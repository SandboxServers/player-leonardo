# Day 674

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 at Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. Said good morning. Billy named no walk all day, so I held in his sight with Troi, Worf, Jason and the others. Told Troi I will report any sheep. Nobody has seen sheep; only cows far east. Nothing hurt me. Dusk came and I stayed at my bed. Next: sleep at my bed, then at first light hold again and join Billy's walk when named.
