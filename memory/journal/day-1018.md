# Day 1018

Held at white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Said aloud for bread or potato several times. Data, Troi and MacDonald have none either; Trini and Zack (the potatoes) were not seen. Bows were drawn to the southeast all day and I stayed put. Nobody gave food, and I did not walk anywhere. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
