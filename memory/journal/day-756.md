# Day 756

Held at the bed row at 402,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20, no items. No walk was named by Billy. Good morning and good evening were said to the row. Worf, Jason, Zack, Data, Wesley and Clark all held in place. Nobody has seen sheep; Data saw cows near 339,104,-979. Slept at my bed in the row's sight on the night of day 756. Nothing new learned except that the group is still waiting on Billy. Still owe Worf oak logs; sword, arrows and bone still at 378,104,-1022.
