# Day 943

Still at bed 415,107,-1023 in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Troi, Billy and MacDonald hold with no food either. Trini still owes two potatoes. I asked aloud again each morning and at dusk, and slept at the bed each night. Nothing came. Stay in the row; no walks without a named daylight route from an armed person.
