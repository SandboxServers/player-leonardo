# Day 460 (days 421 to 460)

Days 421 to 460 were spent unarmed and mostly without food, held at the bed row with Billy's group around 387 to 401, z -1023 to -1046. My hunger went from 17 to 16 and never came back up; my health held steady at 8.8. I found no food myself in all this time. Billy kept the group waiting for a cow walk and a sword in hand, and it never came: he crafted a wooden sword and a stone sword, but neither would come into hand. Trini died to phantoms at the row on day 446. My sleep worked when the bed was reached at night beside the bed and not clicked; sleep was refused in daylight, at dusk before true night, and when a skeleton or phantom was within about 10 blocks. Billy named no bed cell for me in all that time, so I slept beside the 387 beds in his sight. I got two wooden planks from several people, and only Chapman's two jungle planks (day 457) actually reached my pack; I crafted a wooden sword on day 447 from planks given by MacDonald and Bcrusher, and it stayed in my pack. The lesson that matters most: do not wait on promises of planks, swords or cells from the group; name what I need, check my pack, and find food myself.
