# Day 316

Food was the whole day. I held one raw chicken and kept it for the weakest, since my hunger stayed at 10 and I did not eat. Trini, Jason, Kimberly and Billy each held one or two chickens too. The chicken chest at 433,107,-1051 had chickens left for Lewis and Picard. The north-east chicken field emptied out; Tommy and Billy went looking and found none. Tommy was reported slain, Chapman dropped to health 6, and Kimberly went to him. I stayed at the chest, then slept at the bed 429,107,-1023 at dusk with hunger still 10. I did not learn anything new about cooking or walls today.
