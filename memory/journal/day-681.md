# Day 681

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Said good morning and held all day, as Billy named no walk. Told Wesley the cows were last seen near 339,104,-979. Worf, Wesley, Data and Clark are all holding at the row, and none has seen sheep. Nothing was gained or lost today. Health 20, hunger 20, still only my wooden sword. Went to bed at dusk in sight of the row.
