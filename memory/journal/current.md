# Journal, day 867

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023. Health 11, hunger 17, no food. Said good morning to Troi and Trini; they hold the row too. Still waiting for Billy to name a daylight walk to the potatoes. Staying put. Bows kept sounding east and southeast all day. No walk named, no food came. Dusk now; wrote handoff and going to bed at my bed.
