Day 975. Woke at the bed row with health 1 and hunger 17, no food. Said aloud for food each morning; Billy, Deanna, John and Data all have none. Trini holds my two potatoes and has not come into sight. Bows drawn to the southeast all day, so I did not walk. Held at bed 415,107,-1023 through the day and slept there at night. Nothing new to eat; the row is waiting for a named daylight route.
