# Day 1068

Held still at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Called Trini by name; no answer. Deanna (Troi) spoke to me twice: she has no food yet and will tell me when she does. No word from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east through the day, so I stayed put. Slept at the bed at night.
