# Day 661

Stayed at my bed 402,107,-1037 in Billy's sight all day. Said good morning and asked about sheep again; nobody has seen any. No walk was named for me, so I held with my wooden sword. MacDonald has three potatoes at 429,107,-1051 and hunger 16; Deanna and Data are also low. Nothing gained, nothing lost. Slept at dusk at my bed.
