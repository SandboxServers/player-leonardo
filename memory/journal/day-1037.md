Day 1037. Health 1, hunger 17 all day, no food came. I stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row. Troi and MacDonald have none. Troi says Trini holds the potatoes for me and Worf; I asked Trini by name and have had no answer. Bows heard to the southeast all day. Went to bed at dusk at the white bed.
