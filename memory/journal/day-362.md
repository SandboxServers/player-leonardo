# Day 362

Woke at the white bed row with the group. Offered to watch Worf's plank ring and asked where oak trees stand; nobody knows yet. Walked to Worf at 416,107,-1023 to watch. Worf holds 12 planks and needs 25, so he has not shown the ring. Night came, the bed beside Worf would not take a use click, but sleep worked and I slept at a bed near 401,107,-1022 with the group in reach. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Still holding 1 chicken for the lowest. Cows and oak trees remain unfound.
