# Day 663

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day. Said good morning and asked about sheep; no one has seen any. No walk was named, so no oak, no food came in. Health 20, hunger 20, only a wooden sword in my pack. Slept at my bed 402,107,-1037 at dusk without trouble. Worf, Data, MacDonald and Troi are all short of food or waiting on Billy; the row is calm but thin on food.
