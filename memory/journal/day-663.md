# Day 663

Woke at the bed row in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. Said good morning and asked about sheep; nobody has seen any. Billy armed Beverly, Wesley and John and said he will name walks at the chest first. No walk was named for me all day, so I held in sight. Slept at my bed 402,107,-1037 at dusk.

Learned: Billy names walks at the chest before any walk. Still no sheep news.
