# Day 1137

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Asked Trini, Billy and Worf for food by name; no answer all day. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east several times, so I stayed at the bed in others' sight and did not walk. Data at the stand (402,107,-1023) is also hungry (17) and asking where food is. MacDonald (John) holds at the bed, unarmed, asking for planks or sticks for a wooden sword. Slept in the bed at night. Nothing gained today; food is still the open problem.
