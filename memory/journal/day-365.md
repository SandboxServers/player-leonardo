# Day 365

Stayed at the white beds all day with the group in sight, health 20 and hunger 20 throughout. Said I was ready for Billy's cow walk, but Billy had not called it by dusk, so the walk waits for first light. Holding 1 chicken for the lowest. Met Jason, Trini, Billy, Kimberly, Tommy, Deanna, Meriwether, Wesley and Picard by voice and sight, all at the beds. Nothing new was learned about cows or oak. Slept beside Data at the bed near 430,107,-1023; the night was skipped and I am rested.
