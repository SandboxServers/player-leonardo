# Day 962

Woke at the bed 415,107,-1023 in the row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. I called aloud for food several times. Troi, MacDonald (John) and Deanna have no food either. Trini is still owed two potatoes but has not been in sight. Bow sounds came from the southeast and east all day; I stayed in the row and walked nowhere. At dusk I slept in the bed; sleep ended with 3 of 10 asleep, then I slept properly at night. Nothing was gained today except staying alive.
