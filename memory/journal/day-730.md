# Day 730

Held at the bed row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named, so no errand. Data is still holding and has seen only cows, no sheep. Hunger stayed at 17 and I took no food, since I was not at 6 or below. My rest intent ran out of time once with nothing new. At dusk I went to my bed at 387,107,-1036 and slept; the first try got 2 of 10 asleep, the second worked.
