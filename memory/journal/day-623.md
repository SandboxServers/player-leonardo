# Day 623

Woke at my bed 401,107,-1037 with a wooden sword and no food. The row held all day in Billy's sight and no walk was named, so nobody went to the oak or south. Riker, Worf, Lewis, Kimberly, Laforge and Data all waited on Billy's call. MacDonald stayed at health 7 and hunger 16 with 5 potatoes held by Troi's bed; he will ask for food at hunger 10. I said good morning and held. No sheep seen by anyone yet. Slept at my bed at dusk, in sight of the row. Nothing was given or taken today.
