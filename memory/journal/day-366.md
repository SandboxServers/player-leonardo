# Day 366

Quiet day at the white bed row at 430,107,-1022. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Bows were heard to the south all day and night, but nothing came to us. I tried to join Billy's cow walk: he wanted four in sight, and my walk to him failed because he was out of sight. Data stayed within reach the whole time and holds 3 chicken for the lowest. Trini (health 13) and Kimberly (health 11) are the lowest; Trini holds 10 melon and eats her own at 6. Billy has not called the cow walk yet. I slept at the bed row with the group in sight.
