# Day 1042

Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. I stayed at my white bed, 416,107,-1023, in the bed row. No one brought food. Troi and MacDonald are near and also have none; Troi asked Trini to serve me first. Bows were drawn to the SE and E through the day, and I did not walk. I slept at the bed at night. Nothing new was learned; the main thing is that I am still waiting for food.
