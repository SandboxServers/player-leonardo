# Day 1050

Woke at white bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Troi, John and Billy were in the bed row and spoke kindly; Troi said Trini holds the potatoes and I am first, to stay in her sight. I called Trini by name in daylight, but she did not come into sight. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast all day, so I stayed at the bed. At dusk I slept at the bed; I am not enclosed.
