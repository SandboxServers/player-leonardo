# Day 594

Woke at my bed 401,107,-1037 with health 20, hunger 20, holding only my wooden sword. Said good morning; Billy named no walk because no west route is verified. The whole row held at their beds all day. Troi was at hunger 16 and Billy promised his potato at 10. I had no food and took none. Near dusk Trini was offered bed 429,107,-1037, and Wesley's bed 415,107,-1050 refused him ("way blocked"). I slept at my bed at true night in Billy's sight; the sleep was taken. Nothing new was learned about the west route. Trini's sword is still in my pack.
