# Day 1005

Day 1004 into 1005. I stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day and all night, health 1, hunger 17, no food. I called aloud for food at night and at morning. Macdonald, Troi, Data, Zack and John all have no food. Trini, who holds the potatoes, was not in sight. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I stayed put. Nothing moved forward, but I was not hurt and I kept to the rule: walk only on a named daylight call. I slept at the bed at dusk on day 1005.
