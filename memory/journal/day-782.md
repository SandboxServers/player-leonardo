# Day 782

Held at the bed row all day at 402,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no items. Billy named no walk, so I did not move. No sheep seen by me or anyone at the row; cows only, near 339,104,-979. Wesley and Worf asked why the -1051 squares refuse people; no answer yet. Trini still owes me an oak plank. Went to sleep at dusk at the bed, day 783 night.
