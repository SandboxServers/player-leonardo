Day 1105. I stayed at my white bed at 416,107,-1023 all day, health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. I said good morning and asked Trini and Worf by name for a potato; neither answered. Troi came by several times, has no food either, and keeps listening. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east, and no walk call came. Nothing new was learned beyond who still has food and who does not. Slept at the bed at dusk.
