Day 1081. Stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, not enclosed, in sight of the row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Asked Trini by name for a potato in the morning; no reply yet. Bows were drawn to the southeast and south through the day and none reached me. Troi (Deanna) is at her bed with health 20 and an empty pack. Slept at the bed at night.
