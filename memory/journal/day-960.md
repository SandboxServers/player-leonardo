# Day 960

Still at bed 415,107,-1023 in the bed row, health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Asked the row aloud for food several times. Troi answered: she has none, and Trini holds my two potatoes, to be given when I am in sight. Billy is holding for the night and Worf is planning a cell opening toward the potatoes. No walks, no food arrived. Slept at the bed at dusk, day 960.
