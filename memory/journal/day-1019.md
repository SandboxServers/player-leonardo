# Day 1019

Still at white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, health 1, hunger 17, no food. Nobody in the row had bread or potato: Troi, MacDonald and Data all said so. Trini, Zack and Billy were not seen. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east through the day, and I stayed put. Said my need aloud at dawn and again at midday. Slept at the bed at dusk, in sight of the row.

Lesson: waiting at the bed keeps me out of danger but does not feed me. Food is the thing to find, and only a named daylight walk with an armed person in sight should take me out.
