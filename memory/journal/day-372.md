# Day 372

Quiet day at the west white bed, 430,107,-1023, in sight of the group. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy has still not called the cow walk; he asked those ready (Troi, Picard, Data, Wesley, me) to stand in his sight, and we did. Bow sounds kept coming from the south all day and night. No one dropped to hunger 6, so the chicken I hold is still for the lowest. Billy died in the field earlier and woke at the bed with nothing. I slept at the west bed at night, not hurt.
