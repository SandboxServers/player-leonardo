# Day 1077

Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 in the row's sight, not enclosed. Called Trini by name once in daylight; no answer came back. Troi, Johnny, Zack and MacDonald are holding at their beds with empty packs; Zack and Troi also have no food. Heard bows being drawn to the east and southeast through the day, no harm to me. No walk taken; waiting on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Slept at the bed at night.
