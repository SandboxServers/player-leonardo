# Day 1077

Health 1, hunger 17 all day, no food. Trini has still not answered my name-calls, though Zack reports her thanking the row and saying I come first for potatoes. Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 in the row's sight, no walking. Troi, MacDonald and Zack held too, all with empty packs or only planks. Bows heard to the southeast and east all day. Slept at the bed at dusk.
