# Day 403

Walked with Billy's group toward the cow field at 339,104,-979 in daylight. The walk stopped partway; Billy and the rest turned back and I was held at the bed row. The group stayed in sight and held through dusk. I slept in the white bed at 413,107,-1041 with Billy, Tommy, Lewis, Jason and Picard in sight. I hold 1 chicken for the lowest. Health 8.8, hunger 17. Cows still unfound. Worf still in the pit at 394,107,-1022.
