# Day 358

Thunder and dim all day, so Billy held the cow walk. I stayed at the bed row with Troi and Jason in reach, holding 1 chicken for the lowest. Nobody dropped to the food line. Wesley, Picard and Beverly brought beef, melon and food for the lowest. Worf brought white wool for Data. At dusk I slept in the bed at 401,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. No cows found yet.
