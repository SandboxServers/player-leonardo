# Day 1045

Nothing came of the day. I stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, as planned. I called Trini by name in the morning; she did not come into sight, so no potato. Troi and John are at their beds nearby, both with no food. Bows were drawn to the SE and E again and again, none near me. Health 1, hunger 17. I slept at my bed at dusk, not enclosed. Food is still the only thing that matters.
