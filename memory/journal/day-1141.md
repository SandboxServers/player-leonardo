# Day 1141

Stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day. Pack empty, no food known, health and hunger both 20. Nobody came within sight. I asked Billy, Worf and Trini for food by name at dawn and again at dusk, and MacDonald is holding at the bed next door. Bows were heard drawn to the east and southeast all day, so I did not walk. Slept in the bed at dusk.
