# Day 1141

No food all day. Hunger stayed at 20 but the pack is still empty and no food is known. Asked Billy, Worf and Trini aloud for food at dawn and again later; no one who has food answered. Data says the chest holds no bread and he has no food either. MacDonald needs planks or a stick for a sword. Bows were drawn southeast and east, so I stayed at the white bed at 416,107,-1023. Nothing new was learned. Slept at the bed at dusk.
