Day 964. Woke at bed 415,107,-1023 in the row with health 1, hunger 17, no food. Said aloud for food twice; Troi, MacDonald and Billy have none. Trini, who holds my two potatoes, was not in sight. Bows were drawn to the southeast all day and nobody moved. I stayed at the bed, wrote a handoff, and slept at dusk.
