Day 994. Woke at bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food. Spent the day in the bed row asking aloud for food. Troi, MacDonald, Data have none spare. Trini, who holds my two potatoes, never came into sight. Bows were heard to the southeast and east all day, so I stayed put. Nobody harmed me. Health held steady but will not regenerate while hunger is low. Slept at dusk at the bed.
