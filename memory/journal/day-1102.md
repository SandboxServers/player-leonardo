# Day 1102

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. I greeted the row in the morning and asked Trini and Worf for a potato by name. No answer. Troi and John also have no food and are holding. No named walk call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. At dusk I slept at the bed.
