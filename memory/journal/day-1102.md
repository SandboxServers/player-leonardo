# Day 1102

Held at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day, health 1, hunger 17, no food, only my wooden sword. Asked Trini and Worf for a potato by name at dusk and again at morning; no answer from either. Troi, John and Jason also have no food, so the row has none to give. Bows were sounding to the south and east all day, and no one called a walk, so I stayed put. Troi and John spoke of holding for a named call from Billy or Worf. Sleep was refused at dusk, and I slept in my bed once night came. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is still holding and the food is still with Trini and Worf.
