# Day 765

Woke at bed 402,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, no items. Said good morning to the row. Pillagers were reported north-west, so the whole row held still and Billy named no walk all day. No sheep seen by anyone; the question where sheep graze is still open. Worf's hunger fell to 14 with no food in his pack; Zack holds two potatoes and will eat at 18. Riker went to sleep in a bed at 413,107,-1042 away from the pillager. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing was lost today, and nothing was gained beyond what the row shared.
