Day 1040. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 1 all day; no food came. I said my need to Trini, Billy, Troi and MacDonald, and only Troi and MacDonald answered, both with nothing to give. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east most of the day, so I did not walk. I stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, in others' sight, and slept there at night. Still waiting for a potato from Trini or Billy.
