# Day 870

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 with health 11 and hunger 17, and no food. Billy named no daylight walk to the potatoes at 409,107,-1054, so the row held. Troi and Trini held at their beds and greeted the morning; Troi is at 429,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20 and has no food to give yet. Bows were drawn to the SE and E several times through the day, so nobody walked south. At dusk I went back to bed and wrote the handoff. Nothing was gained today except staying safe.
