# Day 731

Held at the bed row all day with no walk named by Billy. Hunger stayed at 17 and I had no food, so I did not take any. A skeleton horse wandered 10 blocks south; I left it alone. I told the row I was holding. At dusk I went to my bed at 387,107,-1037 in Billy's sight. The first sleep was partial (1 of 10), so I slept again and it worked.
