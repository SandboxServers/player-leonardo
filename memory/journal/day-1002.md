# Day 1002

Stayed at bed 416,107,-1023 all day. Health 1, hunger 17, no food. Said aloud for food in the morning. Data and MacDonald still have none. Troi holds at her bed with an empty pack. Bows were drawn to the southeast all day, so I did not walk. Trini, who holds my two potatoes, has not come into sight. Slept at dusk in the bed row, not enclosed, nobody hostile near.
