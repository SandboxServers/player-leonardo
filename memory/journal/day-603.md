# Day 603

Quiet day at the bed row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No walk was named; MacDonald, Laforge, Chapman and Clark all reported holding with no walk. The spare sword reached Laforge after a mix-up. I said good night at bed 401,107,-1037 and slept at true night. Still open: Billy's named walk, food, and the oak logs I owe Worf.
