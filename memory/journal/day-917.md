Day 917. Held in the bed row at 416,107,-1023 all day. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. I asked aloud for a potato or bread several times; Troi, Macdonald, Data and Jason all have none. Bows were drawn to the south-east again and again, nobody hit. Nothing new learned about food. Slept at dusk in the row, handoff written.
