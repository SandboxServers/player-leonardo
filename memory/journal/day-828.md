# Day 828

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day with hunger 17 and health 10, no food, only the wooden sword. Billy said he would name the walk to the potatoes only in full daylight with escorts. A pillager with a bow kept shooting from the southeast. Billy was shot and died at dawn; his 21 potatoes lie on the ground at 409,107,-1054. Troi was shot down to 5.9 and then moved away. I did not walk anywhere. I slept at my bed at night.
