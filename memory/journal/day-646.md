# Day 646

Quiet day at Billy's row, bed 402,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, still only a wooden sword and no food. No walk was named all day; the row held in sight of each other. I greeted the row in the morning and asked about sheep; nobody has seen them yet. Data, MacDonald (health 7, five potatoes) and Deanna (health 14) are the ones most in need. At dusk I slept at my bed with the row in sight. Learned: the row keeps its morning greeting and its waiting rhythm, and the lowest are still MacDonald and Data.
