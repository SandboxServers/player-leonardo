# Day 1056

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, no food. Called Trini by name in daylight, asking for potatoes. Deanna heard me and said she would ask Trini to give me the potatoes first; no answer from Trini yet. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, so I stayed at the bed in others' sight. No one called me to walk, so I did not walk. Slept at the bed through the night. Still hungry, still at health 1.
