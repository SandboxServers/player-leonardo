# Day 1053

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023, health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Called Trini by name in daylight; no reply. MacDonald and Troi both answered: neither has food, and MacDonald says any potato that reaches him goes to me first. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day and nothing reached me. I stayed at the bed as my goals say. Went to sleep at dusk, not enclosed. Hunger is still the thing that matters most.
