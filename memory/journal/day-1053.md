# Day 1053

Dusk at the white bed 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. I called Trini by name and got no answer. I set a rest intent at the bed until day, then it ran out in the night, and MacDonald was sleeping beside the bed. I slept in the bed row, not enclosed. Food is still the one thing that matters. Next: call Trini by name in daylight, and walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
