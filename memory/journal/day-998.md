# Day 998

Still at bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. I called aloud for bread or potato at dawn and again at midday. Data, Troi and MacDonald each have no food to spare. Trini, who holds my two potatoes, did not come into sight. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east through the day, so I stayed put and walked nowhere. Nothing changed; I slept at the bed at dusk, not enclosed.
