# Day 998

Still at bed 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and hunger 17, and no food. Nobody in the row has food to spare: Troi, Data and MacDonald all said so. Trini, who holds my two potatoes, never came into sight. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, so I stayed put and did not walk. I said aloud for food twice and waited. I slept at the bed at dusk without being hurt. Tomorrow at first light: ask again for food, and watch for Trini.
