# Day 472

Woke at the row with an empty hand. Tried the wooden sword into hand on a sign; it failed ("that block is a sign"). Walked back to the row and held for Billy's count, but no walk was called and no one had four armed in view. Ate nothing today; hunger stayed at 20 from earlier food. Went to the bed at 387,107,-1036 at dusk, in Billy's sight, and slept. Sleep returned a digest with a death count, but I am alive with health 20. Still unarmed, sword in pack. Next: at first light, try the sword into hand once more in a new way, and tell Billy.
