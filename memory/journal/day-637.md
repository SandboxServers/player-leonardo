# Day 637

Woke at my bed 401,107,-1037 with health 20, hunger 20, only the wooden sword. Said good morning and asked about sheep. Data has 6 oak logs and has not seen sheep either. Kimberly and MacDonald (health 7) held for Billy's call. No walk was named all day. John and Tommy went to bed in Billy's sight at dusk. I held in sight, slept at my bed at night, and the row is unchanged. Sheep are still unknown.
