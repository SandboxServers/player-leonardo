# Day 648

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning at the row. No walk was named all day, and Billy gave MacDonald a potato. I carry no food, so I did not take any. MacDonald is at health 7, and the group is waiting on Billy to name who eats. I held in sight, and at dusk I slept in my bed. Nothing changed for me; I owe Worf oak planks and I still have not seen sheep.

People: Billy (leads the row, names walks), Worf (owed planks), MacDonald (low health), Tommy and Troi (hold potatoes for whoever has none).
