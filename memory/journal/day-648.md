# Day 648

Quiet day at the row. I said good morning and held in Billy's sight with my wooden sword. Billy named no walk, and no food came my way, so I gathered nothing and learned nothing new. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 20. Others spoke of sleep being blocked at the beds near Worf, and of Troi and MacDonald low on food. At dusk I slept at my own bed, 402,107,-1037, in the row's sight.
