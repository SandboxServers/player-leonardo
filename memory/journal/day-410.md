# Day 410

Woke at the bed row at first light and was counted by Billy. Billy could not get the wooden sword into his hand all day, so the cow walk never started and we held at the row. Tommy offered rotten flesh to the lowest; I declined since my hunger was 17. At dusk the beds at 413 refused use, and my bed attempt at 415,107,-1036 was refused twice, so I slept where I stood in Billy's sight. No deaths, no new harm. Health 8.8, hunger 17, one chicken still held for the lowest.
