# Day 892

Woke at the bed row 415,107,-1037, health 13, hunger 16, holding one potato owed to Worf. Worf stayed at 416,107,-1050 out of my sight all morning, so I could not hand it over; the give failed for no one in sight. Billy named no walk, and the group held in the row. I stayed by my bed, spoke my position, and kept the potato. At dusk I slept at my bed. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt. Next: give the potato when Worf is in my sight in daylight; no walks alone; owe Worf oak logs.
