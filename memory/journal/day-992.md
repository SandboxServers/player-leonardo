# Day 992

Slept at bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, still no food. Asked aloud at dawn for food and for Trini's two potatoes. Data, MacDonald and Troi have none to spare, but all stay in sight and listen for a named route. Bows were heard to the southeast and east all day, so I stayed at the bed. Nothing to eat was found.
