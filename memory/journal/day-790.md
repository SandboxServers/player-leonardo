# Day 790

Stayed at the bed row at 402,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20, no items. No walk was named by Billy and no sheep has been seen. Trini, Jason and Data are holding at the row too; Data is at health 13 and Worf and Jason are short on food, and I have none to share. At dusk I said good evening to the row, and Trini and Jason answered that they were sleeping in Billy's sight. I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new was learned today. Next: wait for Billy's named walk, ask again about sheep and the -1051 squares.
