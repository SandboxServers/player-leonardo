# Day 673

Quiet day at the row. I woke at my bed, said good morning, and held in Billy's sight the whole day. Billy named no walk, so nobody went out for food or oak. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20 for me; Deanna and Data are lower and Trini is at 17. Many voices said good morning and good night, and nobody has yet seen sheep. I slept at my bed at night in Billy's sight; the sleep held.
