Day 1118. Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023 with health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning and asked Trini about potatoes at 423; no answer. MacDonald and Data are also at the bed with no food; Data's chest at 405 has no bread. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, and nobody was hit. I held at the bed and walked nowhere, since no named call came from Billy or Worf. Slept at the bed at night, in others' sight.
