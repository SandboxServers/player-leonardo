# Day 378

Morning at the bed row, health and hunger full. Billy asked me to call the cow walk once four were in my sight. I could see only Data, so I asked the others to step into view. Billy then counted four (Wesley, Trini, Lewis, Picard), and the walk started toward 339,104,-979, but it stopped at 414 when he saw no one. The walk was called off for the day at dusk. Jason, Picard and Beverly came in to the group. I held the chicken for the lowest and slept at the bed 429,107,-1023 in sight of the group. Bow sounds came from the south all day.
