# Day 716

Held at the bed row all day with no walk named by Billy and no food in my pack. Health and hunger stayed full. Said at the row that I was holding and asked Billy about a walk; no answer came. Data also held, has seen only cows, and still no sheep has been seen. At dusk Data went to bed, and I slept at my bed 387,107,-1037 on the night of day 716.
