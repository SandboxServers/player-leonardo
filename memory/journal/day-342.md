# Day 342

Quiet day at the white beds near 430,107,-1037. I held 3 chicken and 2 melon for the lowest, but nobody dropped to 6 except Trini, who stayed at 17 hunger. Jason was never fed by Kimberly because her gives failed when he was out of sight; by dusk Jason had his own raw chicken. The cow spot at 364,-1010 was empty again. At dusk I went to the bed at 429,107,-1037 and slept with the group near, using the sleep action.
