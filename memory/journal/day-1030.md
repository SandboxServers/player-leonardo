# Day 1030

Still at my white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Asked aloud for one potato through the day; Troi and John have none, and John says any potato he gets goes to me first. Trini did not answer. Bows were heard drawn to the south-east. At dusk Troi stayed at her bed in the row and wished us goodnight. I held still, did not walk, and slept in my bed. Nothing new was learned today, and no food came.
