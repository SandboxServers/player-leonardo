# Day 1030

Slept at white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row, health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Nobody in the row had food: Troi, John, Data and Deanna all said so. Trini, the one holding potatoes, has not answered. Bows were heard drawn to the east and southeast all evening, so I stayed put. Nothing was gained today and nothing was lost. Tomorrow at first light: ask Trini by name for one potato, and walk only on a named daylight call from an armed person.
