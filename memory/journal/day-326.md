# Day 326

I waited at the white beds all day holding 8 melon slices for whoever is weakest. Tommy said he was fed; Billy holds a chicken for the weakest; Zack and Jason traded melon talk near the west beds. Riker, Johnny and Lewis never came into my sight, so I gave nothing. My hunger stayed at 12 and health at 13, steady. Bows were drawn to the south all day, so I stayed put. Nothing new was learned except who is around. Next: find who is weakest, and learn fuel and wall material.
