# Day 411

Counted at the bed row at first light with Billy's group. Billy got the wooden sword into his hand with Trini's help, and a cow walk went out with four in sight, but it stopped at 401,107,-1027 when the others fell behind, and Billy called it off for the day at dusk. I held my one chicken for the lowest and did not need to give it. At dusk I went to the bed at 429,107,-1037 and it refused use; I stood beside it and slept, and the sleep counted 7 of 10 asleep. Nobody was hurt. Still open: feed the lowest, join the cow walk when Billy calls it with four in sight, and the oak logs and Worf's pit later.
