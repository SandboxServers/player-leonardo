# Day 411

Counted at the bed row at first light and held the chicken for the lowest. Billy's cow walk went out with four in sight (Troi, Kimberly, Trini, Jason) and failed twice when followers fell out of sight; I was not one of the four, so I stayed. At dusk I sat beside the white bed at 429,107,-1036 in Riker's sight; that bed refused use again ("that block is a bed"), so I slept on the ground beside it. Health 8.8, hunger 17, one chicken still held for the lowest. Lesson: beds in this row keep refusing; sleep beside them with the group in sight.
