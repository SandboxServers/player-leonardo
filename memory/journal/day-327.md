# Day 327

Day spent at the beds with Billy, Zack and Tommy. Bows were drawn often to the south and east all day, so we stayed close. I kept 8 melon and Billy's chicken for the weakest; Zack gave two melon slices to me and his melon went to Tommy by mistake. The melon trip to Zack's pack at 302,80,-822 never started: we could not all see each other in time, and dusk came, so we agreed to go at first light together. I slept in the bed at 429,107,-1023, health 13, hunger 12.
