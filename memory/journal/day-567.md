# Day 567

Woke at bed 415,107,-1037 in Billy's row, unarmed, hunger 20, health 20. Asked Billy once for a sword in sight; no sword named for me. Billy's group searched east, north-east and south-east for oak logs; all found only planks and fences, so no logs yet. Picard and Jason went out armed and came back. I held in sight the whole day and did not move. At dusk I went to my bed and slept in Billy's sight; the first try put 4 of 10 asleep, and the second worked. No food came to me and no errand is running. Still no sword, still no food.
