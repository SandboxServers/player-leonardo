# Day 1080

Slept at white bed 416,107,-1023 at night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Held at the bed all day in the row's sight; bows drawn to the east and south all night, none reached me. Troi greeted the row, health 20, also without food; I told her where I am and that I hold here. No walks taken.
