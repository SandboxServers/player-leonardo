# Day 784

Held at the bed row all day with the group, no walk named by Billy. Only cows seen (near 339,104,-979), still no sheep. Data reported an enderman close by and we did not look at it. Carried nothing; health 20, hunger 20. Went to bed at dusk at bed 402,107,-1037 in the row's sight. Worf, Jason, Wesley, Trini and Beverly all held close, and food is still the group's limit.
