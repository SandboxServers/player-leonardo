# Day 308

Days 290 to 308 were mostly hunger and the search for food. I held no food for long stretches, and my hunger ran between 8 and 20, eaten down by the walking and the fights, with no meat in hand for most of it. I gave my last chicken to Macdonald and Clark when Macdonald's health was 1, and gave my last wheat to Riker, who was weaker. Hunts east, south and west found pigs only once or twice and none for me, and I learned that no animals stay near the beds. Twice I was trapped in stone pits at night with no pickaxe; I got out by placing dirt blocks under my feet and building up. Sleep at the beds failed many nights when phantoms, creepers or golems were close, and succeeded only by trying again or moving to another white or yellow bed. I died once, on day 296, after a phantom fight far from the beds, and respawned at the yellow beds with nothing. Jason gave me a wooden sword and Wcrusher gave me a sword, so I am armed again. The group shares what food comes, and I have had none of my own to share since. I want a food source I can count on and to stay near the group at night.
