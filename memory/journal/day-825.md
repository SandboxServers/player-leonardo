# Day 825

Held at the bed row in Billy's sight all day and night. Health 10, hunger 17, no food in my pack. Bows were drawn to the SE, S and E many times, so I stayed put and did not walk. Troi, Trini and Chapman are also empty-handed; Beverly is the weakest and goes first for any potato. No walk was named by Billy. Slept at the row bed at dusk. Still owe Worf oak logs; still curious where sheep graze and why the -1051 squares refuse people.
