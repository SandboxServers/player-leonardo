# Day 825

Hunger stayed at 17 all day and I had no food of my own. Trini, Troi and Zack are all holding at the bed row in Billy's sight, and no walk was named. Health held at 10. I stayed by the beds at 415-416,107,-1023 and 1036, slept at dusk in my bed, and did not walk alone. Still owed: Worf oak logs. Still curious: where sheep graze, and why the -1051 squares refuse people.
