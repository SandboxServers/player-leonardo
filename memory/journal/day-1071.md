# Day 1071

Stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1023 all day and night. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword only. Called Trini by name at first light on day 1070 and again on 1071; no reply. MacDonald said he would ask her for potatoes for me. Troi holds too, with no food. Bows were drawn to the southeast and east all day, out of sight. Nothing new was learned. Slept at the bed at dusk; the sleep got only part of the way.
