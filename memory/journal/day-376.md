# Day 376

The group held at the white bed row all day, waiting on Billy's cow call. Everyone stayed at full health and hunger. Lewis said he is not hungry, so I kept my one chicken for whoever drops to 6. Billy moved to count us; by dusk he counted four (Lewis, Data, Wesley, Troi) and Picard, but would not walk to the cows in the failing light with no weapon. The walk is set for first light. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1023 in the group's sight. Bow sounds came from the south all day, and nothing reached us.
