# Day 376

Quiet day at the white bed row near 429,107,-1023. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy, Wesley and Troi gathered at the beds for the night. Billy set the cow walk for first light on day 377, counting people by sight. A bow was drawn to the south during the evening. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1023, in the group's sight, and the cows are still unfound.
