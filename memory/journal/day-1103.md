# Day 1103

Woke at the white bed 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Said good morning and asked Trini and Worf for a potato by name. No answer. Troi answered my greeting but has no food; Jason also has no food. Bows were drawn to the southeast most of the day. I held at the bed, did not walk, and waited. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
