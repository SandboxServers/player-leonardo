# Day 963

Dusk to night at bed 415,107,-1023 in the row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Said aloud for food several times; Troi, Worf, MacDonald, Billy and Deanna all hold at their beds with no food to share. Trini still holds my two potatoes and was not in sight. No walk was named, so I stayed in the row. Heard bows drawn to the southeast and east through the night. Slept at the bed at night.
