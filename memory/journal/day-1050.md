# Day 1050

Slept at white bed 416,107,-1023 in the bed row. Health 1, hunger 17, no food all day. Called Trini by name at morning light; she did not come into sight. Troi, Data and MacDonald have no food to spare but are holding at their beds and listening. Bows drawn to the southeast through the day, which I did not walk toward. Nothing hurt me. Waiting for Trini and a daylight call from an armed person.
