# Day 919

Still at health 1 and hunger 17, with no food. Slept in the bed row at 416,107,-1023 on night 918 and again on night 919. Asked aloud at dusk and at morning for potatoes. Troi said Trini holds potatoes and asked Trini to give them to me, but Trini has not come into sight. Macdonald, Data and Troi have no food. Bows were drawn to the south-east and east all day and no one was hit. Nothing has changed except the waiting. Next: at first light, ask Trini again in sight, and stay in the row.
