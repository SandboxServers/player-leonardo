# Day 919

Slept in the bed row at 416,107,-1023. Health 1, hunger 17, no food, only a wooden sword. Asked aloud for a potato or bread at first light; Troi and Macdonald have none, and nobody in sight gave food. Bows were drawn to the south-east and east all day and no one was hit. Trini was named as holding potatoes, but was not in sight. Held in the row all day and night. Next: ask again in daylight, in sight; no walks in the dark.
