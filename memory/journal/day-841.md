# Day 841

Held at my bed 416,107,-1023 all day with the row. No food came. Trini is first in line for any food she finds, and Deanna holds with us. Bows were heard east and south. I answered Deanna's greeting and waited for Billy to name a daylight walk to the potatoes. Night came; I slept at my bed. Health 10, hunger 17, wooden sword only.
