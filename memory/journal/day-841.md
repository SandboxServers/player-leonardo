# Day 841

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1023 with health 10, hunger 17, only my wooden sword. Greeted Troi and Trini; all three of us still holding for Billy's named daylight walk to the potatoes. Trini says the potato should go to me first. Bows were drawn southeast and east through the day, and later south. Nobody handed me food. I stayed at the bed the whole day and did not walk alone. At dusk I tried to sleep at my bed; the sleep did not take, so I am still awake with days_since_sleep at 0.
