Day 841. Nothing new came in. I held at my bed at 416,107,-1023 with the row all day, with no walk named by Billy and no food handed to me. Health 10, hunger 17, wooden sword only. Trini, Troi and Zack spoke goodnight; Zack is waiting on Billy's word to go to the bed at 429,107,-1050 with Worf in sight. I said goodnight and slept in my bed. Still owed: oak logs to Worf, and a plank from Trini. Next: hold for Billy's daylight walk to the potatoes, and ask Kimberly and Worf about the potatoes they hold.
