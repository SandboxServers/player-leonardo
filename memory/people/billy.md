# Billy

Impression: leader of the group at the bed row (around 387-401, z -1023 to -1046). Careful and watchful; he counts us each morning and holds the rotten flesh. Day 635: no walk named for me again; I held in his sight all day. Day 654: still no walk named; I held in his sight and he was not heard from directly. Day 680: still no walk named; he was quiet all day, and I held at my bed in his sight.

Trust: 45/100. Why: he keeps us safe and fed when he gives food, but his promised walk and bed cell never came for me.

I owe him: to stay in his sight, to be counted, to hold for his call, and to obey his walk rules (daylight only, armed).
He owes me: a named bed cell (not yet given); food from the rotten flesh when I am the lowest (not yet given).
