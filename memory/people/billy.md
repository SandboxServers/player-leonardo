# Billy

Impression: leader of the group at the bed row. Careful and watchful; he counts us each morning and calls the walks. Day 876: he named a daylight walk to 430,-1051, held 2 potatoes for me and said he would give them to whoever is weakest. Beverly (health 7) came first by his rule, and Kimberly then gave me 2 potatoes as he asked.

Trust: 60/100. Why: he kept his word on the walk and the food this time, though he gave the potatoes first to Beverly.

I owe him: to stay in his sight, to be counted, to hold for his call, and to obey his walk rules (daylight only, armed).
He owes me: a named bed cell (not yet given).
