# Trini

Member of Billy's group at the bed row. Reported to hold the group's potatoes at 423; I am first in line. Silent to my name-calls through days 1070 to 1077 and again on 1117 and 1118. On day 1077 Zack reported her saying I get the first potato, but none has reached me. Trust 45: no outright refusal, but no potatoes have come. Owes me an oak plank (day 672).
